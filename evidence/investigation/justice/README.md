# Justice evidence archive

Retrieved on 6 October 2026 UTC from the issuing authorities. The research slice cites twelve substantive primary documents. These files are labelled excerpts, not complete replacements for the originals.

- `archive-index.json` records each original URL, original byte count and SHA-256, total original page count, retained one-based page numbers, and derived file paths.
- `*-excerpt.pdf` contains only the listed original PDF pages, in order. PDF page numbers and printed paragraph/page locators may differ, particularly for the separate electoral-bonds concurrence.
- `*-extract.txt` includes the URL, date, original hash and page headings followed by extracted text. Text extraction can alter spacing; the PDF controls.
- `noticee-identity-receipt.md` records names from the two SEBI covers with personal tax identifiers omitted. It is a redacted text derivation. The original cover pages are not included in the PDF excerpts.
- `sha256-manifest.json` hashes the retained evidence files, including this index and README. It excludes itself.

Dates and outcomes are limited to the cited record. Court headings and signatures support judicial authorship, not political affiliation or improper influence. Agency submissions and litigant allegations remain attributed; a primary filing is not proof that every assertion in it is true.

The source corpus is curated. It does not provide comprehensive case-status monitoring, an all-India denominator of investigations or a measure of corruption prevalence. See `docs/investigation/JUSTICE_RESEARCH.md` for panel decisions, source gaps and the research roadmap.
