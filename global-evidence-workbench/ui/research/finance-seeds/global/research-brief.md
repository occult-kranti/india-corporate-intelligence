# Global public assets and finance: bounded research seeds

Cutoff: 15 October 2026. Eight cases, twenty-one opened primary-source records, and 249 inherited country/territory entries. Zero countries are represented as exhaustively researched. This is an additive research packet; existing workbench files were not changed.

## Most useful findings

- Mopani is a documented public-finance/private-investment bridge: a separate zero-interest Zambian government facility helped ZCCM-IH meet a Glencore covenant while Delta/IRH entered with 51%. The USD1.1bn package, government facility and upfront payment are different amounts and stages.
- Eletrobras is capital-market dilution; Ausgrid a partial long-term lease; Piraeus a share sale involving a concession; Aramco a minority offering with direct government control retained at the dated snapshot. They cannot share one undifferentiated “country sold” label.
- Ethiopia’s licensed new operator was supported by equity, an A-loan and guarantee exposure. This packet does not show a sale of Ethio Telecom. Two official equity figures remain unreconciled.
- IFC’s Janngo disclosure demonstrates venture/blended finance, with a serious internal amount inconsistency. No reconciled cash amount is asserted.
- Gates Foundation–Gavi is a charitable pledge comparator, expressly not privatization. Pledge, receipt and utilization must be separate. A chair role is not ownership of a recipient organization.

## What the records do not establish

No common criminal, political or coordinated “nexus” is inferred. Shared institutional names are documentary role intersections only. No private addresses, personal victims or beneficiary data are included. Named professional roles and manager ownership are not generalized into personal wealth, ultimate beneficial ownership, money receipt or control of every portfolio asset.

## Global coverage and map behavior

country-coverage.json reuses the workbench’s 249-entry geography list, which includes territories and is not a count of sovereign states. Six host-country transaction/finance cases are anchored in Brazil, Australia, Greece, Saudi Arabia, Ethiopia and Zambia; two comparator cases are anchored by France fund domicile and US announcement origin. Counterparty mentions do not imply national research coverage. Regional fund mandates do not become fabricated investments on the map.

Map unresearched entries neutrally. Do not encode absence of a case as zero privatization, zero foreign ownership or clean governance. Do not size countries by a sum mixing currencies, guarantee exposure, debt ceilings, share prices, commitments and receipts.

## Hypothesis discipline

The ordinary explanation is authorized asset recycling, financing, market entry or philanthropy on disclosed terms. A misconduct explanation would require discriminating evidence such as undisclosed related parties, noncompliant approvals, concealed payment flows or an adjudicated finding. A measurement explanation includes different dates, gross versus net amounts, guarantees mistaken for cash, and separate legal entities merged by name. Current records establish the specified relationships and gaps, not intent. No systemic frequency or causal estimate is supportable from these purpose-selected seeds.
