# Finance page implementation and review

Snapshot: 15 October 2026. This is a separate additive implementation, not a deployment.

## Content

15 purposively selected cases, 39 typed relationships, 45 source records, 48 claim assertions and 249 country/territory coverage entries. Source counts are not counts of independent confirmations. Coverage is explicit and incomplete.

India: IDBI buyer approval; closed Air India, NINL and FSNL transactions; and the named historical CEL, Pawan Hans and BPCL cancelled processes. International cases: Eletrobras, Ausgrid, Piraeus, Aramco, Ethiopia telecom finance, Mopani, IFC–Janngo venture-fund investment and Gates Foundation–Gavi pledge. Finance and philanthropic comparators remain explicitly distinct from privatization.

The inspector preserves transaction stage, source-supported date, price/amount type, original currency/unit, party roles, government/ministry context, ownership assertions, financing limits, source trail and missing joins. No grand total or multi-hop causal/beneficial-owner inference is generated. Country references are schematic orientation points, not asset or personal locations. Every country/territory can be selected, including those with no researched cases.

## Material evidence distinctions

- IDBI stays `bidding`: the 14 October 2026 selected-buyer approval does not establish a definitive agreement or closing. INR 52,878.0806181 crore covers the combined GoI/LIC stake, not government-only receipts. No unnamed lender is invented.
- Watsa/Fairfax voting rights are not converted to economic ownership or a personal IDBI holding.
- Ausgrid is `closed` at month precision, December 2016, after the independent reviewer found issuer confirmation. The 20 October accepted proposal remains separate. It is a 99-year partial lease, not a freehold sale.
- Janngo uses the explicitly reported signed status; its investment/date fields and USD6m-versus-USD48m disclosure conflict remain visible. No reconciled cash total is asserted.
- Ethiopia equity, loan and guarantee instruments remain distinct; the first-loss layer is labeled as included within the guarantee.
- Mopani's package ceiling, component amounts and government loan are separate; the USD135m loan qualification includes covenant interest.
- Gavi is a five-year pledge comparator, not privatization, loan, equity ownership or confirmed cash receipt.
- Cancelled labels are bounded to the named dated processes; they do not classify all later processes.
- Named public officials appear only as dated portfolio/institutional context.
