THE RESEARCH IS NOT FINISHED

Coverage is part of the record.

Questions and alternatives are kept separate from documented facts. No joins are inferred from proximity, name similarity or shared appearances.

An ownership link is not a finding of control or wrongdoing.

Ministers and governments are identified only as dated institutional context. Their office does not establish personal involvement, blame or benefit. A lender is not necessarily an owner; a parent company is not necessarily an ultimate beneficial owner. Unknown financing, ownership and coverage remain visible. Geographic points are coarse country references, never private addresses or transaction sites.
