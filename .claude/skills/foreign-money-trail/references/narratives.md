# Foreign-money fleets — the calibrated narratives ladder

Every `narratives` entry in `research/raw/finance/*.json`, `research/raw/ngo/*.json` and `research/raw/capital/*.json` (asOf 2026-09-26), generated verbatim from the files by the generator recorded at the foot of this page — the claim, the fleet's status, the strongest counter and what would change it. The ladder: established → well-supported → contested → speculative → unsupported → debunked. The fifth column is the cross-examiner's verdict on that narrative from the fleet's `AUDIT.json` (`refuted` true/false, the recommended tier and the first sentence of the reason): **where the AUDIT refuted a narrative, the status in column three is the fleet's pre-audit status and the AUDIT column says what to change** — the raw files were not rewritten. The strongest case and the sources are in the file. Do not edit a figure here; fix it in the file and regenerate.

The one finding that governs the whole ladder: every actor-selecting narrative (BlackRock, "the Rothschilds", Soros, "the World Bank dictates", "BJP-run states get the loans", "the Modi government strangles critics") produced an equally alarming picture when the identical lens was run on the declared control — LIC and the sovereign funds; Citi and Kotak; the state broadcaster's 48-hour response; the UPA-era DPLs and the 1991 package; Kerala and Tamil Nadu above parity; the largest FCRA exits falling on apolitical service charities. That is a finding about the lens, and the fleets calibrated the narratives accordingly.
