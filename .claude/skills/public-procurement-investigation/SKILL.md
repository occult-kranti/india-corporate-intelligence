# Public procurement investigation

Locally authored for ICIP on 2026-10-06. This is a reusable repository workflow, not a downloaded or externally installed plugin. Read `evidence-tiering`, `graph-schema` and `pattern-discipline` alongside this skill.

## Start from a falsifiable question

Write the proposed observation, its time window, jurisdiction, comparison population and evidence that would refute it. A repeated title, single bidder, large contract, political donation, family relation or short graph path is not by itself misconduct.

Record the source actually read: original URL, publisher, publication date, retrieval date, page/table/paragraph, observed period and retrieval status. Retain a hash and faithful excerpt when practical. An unavailable PDF or a search snippet is a discovery lead, not primary evidence. Public access does not automatically confer an open redistribution licence; preserve the publisher and terms.

## Keep procurement stages separate

1. Notice or invitation: proposed purchase, not an awarded contract.
2. Corrigendum, cancellation and re-tender: version history, not repeated completed work.
3. Bid, evaluation and award: separate participant, eligible bidder and winning contractor.
4. Contract and work order: distinguish package, lot, asset, chainage and time period.
5. Measurement book, invoice and payment: retain accounting stage and currency.
6. Completion, independent test and maintenance: establish physical delivery from relevant records.

Do not add estimates, awards and payments into one total. Procurement under a concession may involve a private operator paying a public authority; never infer cash direction from the word contract.

## Review repeated work

Require exact authority, locality, asset/chainage, scope, contract identifiers and overlapping work periods. Remove duplicate extracts; link amendments to their original notice. Check cancellation, failed procurement, phased construction, maintenance, disaster repair, defective-work recovery and warranties before publishing a repeat-work question. Publish eligible records and comparisons as denominators. If asset-level records are unavailable, publish the missing fields and a reproducible method; do not claim to have scanned them.

## Build lawful-scope and time tests

Use the rule that applied on the event date, distinguishing publication from commencement. Record central/state jurisdiction, works/goods/services category, financial threshold, exemption and later amendment. A procurement manual is not automatically a statute. Defence acquisition and state recruitment rules have separate regimes. An unknown event or effective date cannot prove a breach.

## Resolve graph identities

- A company needs its exact legal name plus a CIN, exchange filing, statutory ownership schedule or similarly decisive identity record. Parent, subsidiary, SPV, trust and consolidated reporting perimeter are different entities.
- A person needs a public office/tenure, constituency, DIN or other suitable public professional identifier. A name match is insufficient; avoid private identifiers and home addresses.
- An official holding a portfolio does not establish involvement in every award by its agencies. Do not create minister-to-contractor edges from shared state or ministry.
- For electoral bonds use the original purchase and redemption records and a validated prefix-plus-number join. Do not merge by amount/date or names alone. Party finance and private investment are different instruments.
- Preserve directed predicates, dates, citations, tier and limitations. Publish unknown dates. Draw every unresolved identity without relationship edges, or omit it from the evidence graph.

Consolidated accounts cannot identify the subsidiary that made a payment unless the underlying statement does. A financial statement may document what a company reports; preserve self-reported or unaudited limitations in the source metadata.

## Findings and responses

Attribute an audit finding to the auditor. Display the auditee's explanation, recovery, appeal, stay or later order with comparable prominence. A criminal allegation needs its response and procedural status. Neither an audit qualification nor an investigation establishes guilt. A descriptive graph path has no corruption score, centrality-as-influence label or causal conclusion.

## Panel and implementation gates

Use independent research, sector, legal-methodology, data and design roles. Round one fixes scope and comparison criteria. Round two tests the assembled sources, arithmetic, date logic, identities and actual user journey; record changed decisions. Identify simulated agent roles honestly.

The shipped interface must expose current filter scope, evidence gaps and dates, allow shareable focus and one/two-step neighbourhoods, and provide a keyboard-accessible table with the same records as the graph. Export provenance and limitations with data. A selected entity outside the filtered set must be visible as an invalid focus, never silently replaced by a different entity.

Run schema validation, exact-source controls, chronology and false-positive tests, filter/URL/browser checks and source-equivalent exports. Local embedding models may rank public summaries for discovery. Record model version, licence, hashes, corpus hash and actual run; similarity never verifies a claim or creates a published relationship.
