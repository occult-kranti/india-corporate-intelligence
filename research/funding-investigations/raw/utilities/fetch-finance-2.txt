AESL: Executive Summary – Performance in Q1 FY25

Other Key Recent Updates:

– FTSE Russell has upgraded AESL’s ESG score in the FTSE4Good index from 4 to 4.4 driven mainly by improvement in environment score

– Adani Electricity launched new “Network Operations Center (NOC)” powered by India's first fully functional Advanced Distribution Management System (ADMS) and SCADA technology to ensure reliable power supply to over 12 million consumers

– Completed acquisition of Mahan - Sipat transmission assets, adding 673 ckm to AESL’s total operational network
