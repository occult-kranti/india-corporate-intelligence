GOVERNMENT OF KERALA 
Abstract 
Water Resources Department - ADB Assisted KUWSIP - Water Supply 
System Operations and Maintenance and System Upgrade in Kochi for 
implementing 24 x 7 water supply system in Kochi Municipal Corporation 
- Release of Funds for Mobilization Advance - Released – Orders issued. 
WATER RESOURCES (WS-B)DEPARTMENT 
ORDER 
As per Government order read as 1st paper above, Government of Kerala 
has accorded sanction for the ADB assisted KUWSIP for comprehensive 
Water Supply System Operations & Maintenance and System Upgrade to 
implement a 24x7 Continuous water supply system in Kochi Municipal 
Corporation at a total outlay of Rs.2511 Cr. The project agreement was 
signed on 22/05/2025 with M/s Suez Projects Pvt Ltd for an amount of 
Rs.1141.28 Crore. 
2) As per letter read as 2nd paper above, Managing Director, Kerala 
Water Authority has requested to allot an amount of Rs.132.68 Cr (Rs.75 Cr 
as full budget provision and Rs.57.68 Cr as additional authorization). 
3) Government have examined the proposal in detail and are pleased to 
release an amount of Rs. 75 Crore (Seventy Five Crore) in the heads of 
account 4215-01-101-94 for ADB assisted Kerala Urban Water Supply 
improvement Project - KUWSIP (EAP) subject to the conditions mentioned 
below: 
1. The amount released shall be transfer credited to PSTSB account in 
compliance of GO(P) No.62/2018/Fin dated 16/04/2018. 
2. The instructions in Circular No.84/2008/fin dated 09/12/08, Circular 
No.8/2018/fin dated 30/01/2018 & G.O(Ms) No.93/2020/Fin dated 

WS-B3/227/2025-WRD G.O.(Rt)No.817/2025/WRD

11.09.2020 shall be scrupulously followed. 
3. The Managing Director, Kerala Water Authority shall furnish duly 
authenticated detailed component wise statement of expenditure and 
Utilization Certificate as per instructions in GO.(Ms)No.4/2020/Fin 
dated 09/01/2020. 
4. If there is un-utilized amount at the end of this financial year that 
should be refunded to the government during the financial year itself.
