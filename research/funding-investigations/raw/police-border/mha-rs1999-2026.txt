       The proposals of the State/UT governments to strengthen emergency

response systems and citizen-centric policing are also considered under the

scheme of ‘Assistance to States and UTs for Modernization of Police

(ASUMP)’.




       The Safe City Project was launched as a pilot initiative during 2018-19

in eight cities, namely, Ahmedabad, Bengaluru, Chennai, Delhi, Hyderabad,

Kolkata, Lucknow and Mumbai. While minimum desirable components, such

as smart surveillance, safe public transport and GIS-based crime mapping,

were     prescribed, the    cities were    provided    flexibility   to   decide key

components based on their local requirements and existing infrastructure,

while adhering to the common objective of enhancing women's safety and

curbing crimes against women.




       The Project is in the final stage of implementation. The entire Central

Share has been released in respect of six cities, namely, Ahmedabad,
                                        -3-
                                                R.S.US.Q.NO. 1999 FOR 05.08.2026

Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata, Lucknow and Mumbai. As per the latest

progress reports received from the cities, over 87% of the sanctioned

surveillance   infrastructure,   including     CCTV   cameras    and   Integrated

Command and Control Centres (ICCCs), has been made operational. The city-

wise status of the components established under the Project is placed at

Annexure.




     As observed in the independent evaluation of the Safe City Project

conducted by the Development Monitoring and Evaluation Office (DMEO), NITI

Aayog, the Project has strengthened emergency response systems and

citizen-centric   policing   through   technology-enabled       surveillance   and

command infrastructure. The evaluation has also noted that dedicated

women safety initiatives, such as Rani Chennamma Squad in Bengaluru,

Nirbhaya Squads in Mumbai and Lucknow, and She Teams in Ahmedabad,

have enhanced police visibility and facilitated prompt response to incidents

relating to women's safety.
