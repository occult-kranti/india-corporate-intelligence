## Daily Excelsior, 5 May 2026
https://www.dailyexcelsior.com/nia-court-frames-charges-against-let-trf-handler-harbourers/
Read full article via Exa. Reports a 4 May Special NIA Court, Jammu order in RC02/2025/NIA/JMU allowing charges against a handler and two alleged harbourers on prima facie material. Its final three substantive paragraphs specifically say the two local defendants were not shown to have conspired in the actual execution but were to face harbouring/omission-to-report charges. This is important counterevidence to conflating harbouring and executing the attack. It describes the prosecution's asserted ₹3,000 cash given by one attacker to Parvaiz Ahmad; no bank record, payer identity for that cash leg, source-of-funds ledger or certified court order was inspected. Technical/forensic claims are prosecution material described by the reporter, not independently verified forensic findings. Article names defence counsel but does not reproduce a detailed merits response. No full chargesheet, underlying exhibits or final conviction was obtained.
