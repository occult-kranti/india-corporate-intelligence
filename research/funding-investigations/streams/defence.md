## Money and identity controls

The pack does not compute a graph-wide cash total. Parent and child allocations overlap. Offset obligations, claims and industrial credits are distinct from cash paid to the Government. The three oversight edges for claims, disposal and imposed penalties intentionally carry null structured amounts; their labels and source passages retain the exact reported numbers without turning them into receipts.

The Agusta payment of ₹1,620 crore and guarantee recovery of ₹2,068 crore are explicitly **secondary reported cash stages**. A company statement says SBI and Deutsche Bank AG paid a combined roughly €28m of bonds, but it does not allocate that figure between them. Each bank edge therefore has a null amount. The company's 2019 financial filing distinguishes later enforcement permissions, delivered-aircraft protections and unresolved balances. These sources cannot establish a net fiscal gain or final loss without matched currency/date vouchers and settlement records.

The alleged €42m umbrella is not added to the €6.05m component or €18.2m order. The purported Pawan Hans buyback is an unresolved edge, since the prosecution asserted that no engagement occurred. No unnamed entity is assigned a politician, beneficial owner or payment recipient. No budget increase, vendor benefit or procurement failure is treated as evidence of a staged attack.
