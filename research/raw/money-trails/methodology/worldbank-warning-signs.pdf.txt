procurement. If you spot any of these warning signs (also known as “red flags”) in a World Bank Group (WBG)
funded project, there may be reason for concern. For complaints related to possible fraud or corruption on
WBG-funded projects please contact the Integrity Vice Presidency (INT) using the online form provided on
the WBG public website: https://www.worldbank.org/integrity
