Declining Enrolment of Students in Government Schools in Uttar Pradesh 
 
†2445. Ms Iqra Choudhary: 
 
Will the Minister of EDUCATION be pleased to state: 
 
(a) the number of Government primary, upper primary, secondary and higher secondary schools that have 
been closed or merged across the country from the year 2014 up to June 30th, 2026, State-wise/UT-wise; 
(b) whether the enrolment rate of students in Government schools reported a continuous decline across 
the country during the said period; 
(c) if so, the outcome of any central study conducted in this regard; 
(d) the details of student enrolment rate in Government  schools and data of schools closed or merged in 
Kairana Lok Sabha Constituency during the last five years, year-wise and block-wise; and 
(e) the total budget allocated and spent under Samagra Shiksha Abhiyan and PM -Shri Yojana to check 
the decline in enro lment rate, strengthen basic infrastructure and improve quality of education in 
government schools of Saharanpur and Shamli districts during the last three years? 
 
ANSWER 
 
MINISTER OF STATE IN THE MINISTRY OF EDUCATION 
(SHRI JAYANT CHAUDHARY) 
 
(a): The total number of government schools, as reported by the State/UTs, on the Unified District 
Information System for Education Plus (UDISE+) from year 2014-15 to 2025-26, are at annexure-I. 
 
(b) and (c): As per UDISE+, Gross Enrolment Ratio (GER) at Upper Primary (VI-VIII), Secondary (IX-
X) and Higher Secondary (XI -XII) for the year 2016 -17 is 88.1, 76.4 and 43.8  respectively and for the 
year 2025-26 is 89.6, 81.5 and 61.7 respectively. 
 
(d): As per UDISE+, number of government schools in Shamli district in  Uttar Pradesh from the year 
2021-22 to 2025-26 is given below: 
District 
Total Government Schools

Shamli, Uttar Pradesh 621 621 622 623 623 
 
(e): The Department of School Education &  Literacy is implementing the Centrally Sponsored Scheme 
of Samagra Shiksha in partnership with all the States and UTs. Financial assistance is provided to all 
States and UTs for various components for implementation of the interventions under Samagra Shik sha 
as per norms. Approved Central Releases and the Actual Central Share released under Samagra Shiksha  
for Uttar Pradesh for the year 2023-24 is 664429.01 lakh and 427645.13 lakh respectively, for the year 
2024-25 is 697621.18 lakh and 626479.22 lakh respectively and for the year 2025 -26 is 589243.27 lakh 
and 562215.15 lakh respectively . The details of central share of approved PAB outlay  and total central 
share released under the PM SHRI scheme  to the State of Uttar Pradesh during FY 2023- 24 are 242.99 
crore and 121.5 crore respectively, during FY 2024-25 are 402.68 crore and 246.86 crore respectively and 
during FY 2025-26 are 377.63 crore and 236.22 crore respectively.
