Quota for EWS Seats in Private Unaided Schools 
 
1260. Shri P C Mohan: 
 
Will the Minister of EDUCATION be pleased to state: 
 
(a) the number of seats reserved and filled under the 25 per cent quota for 
economically weaker sections in private unaided schools in Karnataka, including 
Bengaluru, during each of the last five years and whether this represents growth o r 
decline from the earlier average of about 1.5 lakh admissions annually; 
(b) the impact of Karnataka's 2018 amendment introducing the neighbourhood school 
exemption on RTE admissions in the State; 
(c) whether reimbursement of per-child cost to private schools in Karnataka under the 
Scheme is being released in a timely manner and the amount pending, if so, the 
details thereof; and 
(d) the steps being taken by the Central Government to ensure States including 
Karnataka do not dilute the RTE quota for underprivileged children? 
 
ANSWER 
 
MINISTER OF STATE IN THE MINISTRY OF EDUCATION 
(SHRI JAYANT CHAUDHARY) 
(a) to (d): The Right of Children to Free and Compulsory Education (RTE) Act, 2009 
mandates the appropriate Government to provide free and compulsory element ary 
education to every child of the age 6 to 14 years in a neighbourhood school. 
‘Education’ is in the Concurrent List of the Constitution and majority of schools are 
under the jurisdiction of the respective State Government and Union Territory 
Administration which are the appropriate Government for implementation of the 
provision of the RTE Act, 2009.  
Section 12(1)(c) of ‘The RTE Act , 2009  provides for admission of children 
belonging to weaker sections and disadvantaged groups in Class I (or below) in the  
schools specified in sub -clauses (iii) and (iv) of clause (n) of section 2 of the Act to

the extent of at least 25 percent of the strength of that class. For implementation of 
the provision of the Act, the respective State Governments / UT Administrations notify 
the disadvantaged groups and weaker sections and per child cost as per the laid 
down procedure. Section 12(2) of the RTE Act, 2009 provides for reimbursement to 
the private unaided schools for admitting children under Section 12(1)(c) of the Act.  
The Centrally Sponsored Scheme of Samagra Shiksha, provides financial 
assistance to States and Union Territories for various activities/works including 
reimbursement of expenditure incurred for admissions made under Section 12(1)(c) 
of the Act, in accordance with the norms of the scheme.  
As per information provided by the State Government of Karnataka, the 
Karnataka Right of Children to Free and Compulsory Education (Amendment) Rules 
2018 were notified to provide that admission under Section 12(1)(c) would  be 
available only where there is no Government or aided school in the defined 
neighbourhood offering the relevant class.  
The reimbursement of per-child cost to private schools for students enrolled in 
all elementary classes under RTE Act in Karnataka is being released in a timely 
manner as informed by the State Government.
