August 03, 2026 
Public Notice 
 
 
Facility for investors of PACL Ltd. to check status of/deficiency in claim 
applications and making good those deficiencies relating to bank account-  
Extension of Timeline.  
 
 
1. The Justice (Retd.) R. M. Lodha Committee has been refunding to investors 
of PACL. However, certain applications could not be processed on account 
of one or more deficiencies. 
 
2. Vide public notice dated May 07, 2026, the Committee had provided 
opportunity to investors/applicants whose claim applications were found 
deficient due to Bank verification failure, to make good the deficiencies in 
respect of Bank account, by logging in on https://www.sebipaclrefund.co.in. 
The above facility was  made available to investors/applicants from May 07, 
2026 to August 06, 2026. 
 
3. With a view to provide opportunity to maximum number of investors, it has 
been decided to extend the bank account rectification window   up to 
October 06, 2026.  In view thereof, investors/ applicants may resubmit the 
rectified application till October 06, 2026 
 
 
 
Nodal Officer cum Secretary 
Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee 
(In the matter of PACL Ltd.)
