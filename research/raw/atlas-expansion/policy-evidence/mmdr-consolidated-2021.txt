 MINES AND MINERALS (DEVELOPMENT AND REGULATION) ACT, 1957 
(67 OF 1957) 
 
An Act to provide for the  1[development and regulation of mines and minerals]  under the 
control of the Union. 
 
Be it enacted by Parliament in the Eighth Year of the Republic of India as follows:- 
 
 
PRELIMINARY 
Short title, extent and commencement. 
 
1. (1) This Act may be called the Mines and Minerals  2[(Development and Regulation)]  Act, 
1957. 
 
(2) It extends to the whole of India. 
 
(3) It shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may, by notification in  the 
Official Gazette, appoint. 
 
Declaration as to the expediency of Union control. 
 
2. It is hereby declared that it is expedient in the public interest that the Union should take under  
its control the regulation of mines and the development of minerals to the extent herein  after 
provided. 
 
Definitions. 
3. In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires,-

concession granted for the purpose of undertaking prospecting operations followed by  mining
