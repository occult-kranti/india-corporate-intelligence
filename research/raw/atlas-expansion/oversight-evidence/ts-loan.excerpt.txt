The above indicates that some of the major funding sources of Government’s crucial socio economic schemes/ projects are beyond the oversight and control of Legislature. Since the State Government did not disclose the sources, purpose and extent of these borrowings fully in budget documents and the information furnished to audit by the PSUs/ABs is incomplete, it is recommended that State Government may fully disclose all the Off-Budget Borrowings in the budget documents as recommended by the XV FC. It may also pronounce a specific reform path for removal of such indirect borrowings within a time frame, as recommended by XV FC.
