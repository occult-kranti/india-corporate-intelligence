Project Description
The proposed transaction entails an equity investment of up to INR 6,000 million (approximately US$73 million) in the form of compulsorily convertible instruments in a proposed new wholly owned subsidiary (“Mahindra LMM” or the “Company”) of Mahindra & Mahindra Limited (“M&M” or the “Sponsor”). This subsidiary will be newly incorporated to house M&M’s last mile mobility business (LMM). Mahindra LMM plans to incur a total capex of US$113 million over the next 3-4 years towards development and manufacturing of its portfolio. IFC will finance US$73 million which will go towards capex, working capital requirements, etc.  for the electric vehicle (EV) business of LMM, by way of an equity investment in the form of compulsorily convertible instruments (the Project).

Project Sponsor and Major Shareholders of Project Company
The Sponsor, M&M, is the flagship company of the Mahindra Group which is one of India’s oldest and most reputed business groups with a footprint in 100+ countries and ~260,000 employees.
Total Project Cost and Amount and Nature of IFC's Investment
The total Project capex is approximately US$113 million. IFC will fund up to approximately US$73 million, which will go towards capex, working capital requirements, etc.  in the form of compulsorily convertible instruments.

Development Impact
Anticipated Impact Measurement & Monitoring (AIMM) Assessment
The most significant expected project-level outcomes are income and livelihood effects for customers. The Project will increase access to E-3Ws (including passenger and cargo delivery vehicles) across India, supporting income and livelihoods of micro-entrepreneurs and will also contribute to localized air pollution benefits for the drivers of the E-3W vehicles. Beyond the Project, IFC expects the investment to contribute to the integration of advanced EV manufacturing capabilities, the scaling-up of the EV ecosystem in India, and the smooth transition of the auto industry from fossil fuel powered vehicles to EVs.
IFC's Role and Additionality
IFC's additionality stems from (i) financing structure in an evolving sector, (ii) standard setting of governance frameworks and environmental and social (E&S) practices in a newly created company, in discussion with M&M and in consonance with M&M’s existing corporate governance and ESG standards, and (iii) knowledge transfer by leveraging the World Bank Group's expertise in the EV sector and potentially addressing gender gaps in employment.
E&S Category Rationale / Risks and Mitigation
Environmental & Social Categorization Rationale
This is a Category B project as per IFC’s Policy on Environmental and Social Sustainability. Key E&S risks associated with the project are typical risks associated with any manufacturer of electric vehicles including EVs for last mile mobility and includes: i) the new Company’s management and monitoring systems to assess and manage E&S risks and impacts from construction and operation of the new production facility; ii) consistent management of labor and working conditions and occupational health and safety (OHS) policies and practices, workers engaged by third parties and supply chain; iii) resource efficiency, pollution prevention, hazardous waste (including e-wastes and battery wastes) and management of hazardous materials; iv) community health and safety risks including EV safety, traffic & transport, and security personnel; and v) stakeholder engagement and the associated community grievance mechanisms.
