             DEENDAYAL DIVYANGJAN REHABILITATION SCHEME

241.   Shri Ananta Nayak:
       Smt. Mala Rajya Laxmi Shah:
       Shri Alok Sharma:
       Shri Manoj Tiwari:
       Shri Jaswantsinh Sumanbhai Bhabhor:
       Shri Pradeep Kumar Singh:
       Dr. Sanjay Jaiswal:

  Will the Minister of SOCIAL JUSTICE AND EMPOWERMENT be pleased to state:

(a) the details of the progress made in various States under Deendayal Disabled
Rehabilitation Scheme (DDRS) alongwith details of voluntary organisations (NGOs), special
schools and beneficiaries involved under the scheme, during the last three years, State-wise
including Gujarat and Madhya Pradesh;

(b) the details of funds allocated, released and utilised under the Scheme, State-wise
including Gujarat and Madhya Pradesh, specially for Dahod district;

(c) whether the Government has taken steps to promote awareness and participation of
voluntary organisations in tribal and rural areas of the country including Dahod district;

(d) if so, the details thereof and if not, the reasons therefor State and district-wise including
Dahod;

(e) the specific measures adopted to provide access to rehabilitation facilities, early
intervention and community based support services to Divyangjans in remote areas, specially
in Madhya pradesh; and

(f) whether any projects have been sanctioned under DDRS in the country particularly in
Keonjhar Lok Sabha Constituency of Odisha, if so, the details of institutions supported, funds
released, beneficiaries covered, rehabilitation and early intervention services provided and the
steps proposed to expand such services in the constituency, State-wise including such
constituency of Odisha?

         MINISTER OF STATE FOR SOCIAL JUSTICE AND EMPOWERMENT
                            (SHRI B. L. VERMA)


(a) & (b): Under the Deendayal Divyangjan Rehabilitation Scheme (DDRS), financial
assistance is provided to voluntary organisations for providing rehabilitation services to
persons with disabilities, including early intervention, development of daily living skills,
education and training with a view to facilitating their inclusion in the mainstream of society.
The State/UT-wise details of funds released under the Scheme, including in the States of
Gujarat and Madhya Pradesh, are given at Annexure-I.
The details of funds released under DDRS in Dahod District during the last three years are as
under:

       Year                   Amount (₹)
    2023-24                    46,20,561
    2024-25                    17,52,181
    2025-26                         -


(c) & (d): The Department undertakes various awareness generation and outreach initiatives
to enhance awareness regarding disability-related schemes and services, including those
implemented through voluntary organisations. Under the Awareness Generation and Publicity
(AGP) Scheme, one Purple Fair event was organised in Dahod, Gujarat through Composite
Regional Centre (CRC), Ahmedabad in January, 2026. The Department also supports
awareness generation through its National Institutes (NIs) and Composite Regional Centres
(CRCs), including National Institute of Mental Health Rehabilitation (NIMHR), Sehore, and
CRCs at Bhopal and Chhatarpur in Madhya Pradesh. The State-wise details of Voluntary
Organizations/ NGOs to whom Grant-in-Aid (GIA) has been released under the AGP Scheme
are enclosed as Annexure-II.
(e): The Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (DEPwD) implements the
Cross Disability Early Intervention Centre (CDEIC) sub-scheme under the Scheme for
Implementation of Persons with Disabilities Act (SIPDA). Under the Scheme, 28 CDEICs
have been approved in National Institutes and Composite Regional Centres across the country
to provide screening, early identification, referral, therapeutic intervention and parental
counselling for children in the age group of 0–6 years. Out of these, three CDEICs are
functioning in Madhya Pradesh at NIMHR, Sehore, Atal Bihari Vajpayee Training Centre for
Disability Sports (ABVT-CDS), Gwalior and CRC, Bhopal. The Department also facilitates
rehabilitation services through National Institutes and Composite Regional Centres, including
special education, vocational training, skill development, accessible learning resources,
assistive devices and community-based rehabilitation services. In addition, the National Trust
implements schemes relating to early intervention, day care, residential care and health
insurance for persons with Autism, Cerebral Palsy, Intellectual Disability and Multiple
Disabilities, including in Madhya Pradesh.
