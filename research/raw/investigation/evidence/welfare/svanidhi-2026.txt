LOANS SANCTIONED AND DISBURSED UNDER PM-SVANIDHI SCHEME

NO. 695 SHRI TANGELLA UDAY SRINIVAS:

Will the Minister of HOUSING AND URBAN AFFAIRS be pleased to state:
(a) the number of street vendor loans sanctioned and disbursed under
PM SVANidhi scheme during the last five years along with the amount
disbursed and the repayment rate, year and State/UT-wise;
(b) the number of vendors who have availed the second and third
tranches of credit indicating improved creditworthiness, State-wise;
(c) whether any assessment of the impact on income enhancement and
digital payment adoption among street vendors has been conducted
under the scheme and if so, the findings thereof; and
(d) the steps taken to expand PM SVANidhi coverage to coastal market
towns and fishing harbours in Andhra Pradesh including Kakinada
where a significant share of informal street vendors operate?


                               ANSWER
                    THE MINISTER OF STATE IN THE
               MINISTRY OF HOUSING AND URBAN AFFAIRS
                         (SHRI TOKHAN SAHU)

(a) Prime Minister Street Vendor’s AtmaNirbhar Nidhi (PM SVANidhi)

Scheme was launched on 1st June, 2020 with the objective of facilitating

collateral free working capital term loans to street vendors. As on 12th

July, 2026 the scheme has successfully disbursed 1.15 crore loans,

amounting to ₹18475.06 Crore, to 76.95 lakh street vendors. State wise

information under the scheme during last 5 year is provided in Annexure

– I. Repayment rate varies according to the term of the loan. The PM

SVANidhi loan repayment schedule provides for three tranches in 12

months, 18 months, and in 24 months respectively through monthly

instalments.

(b) State-wise number of vendors who have availed the second and third

tranches of credit under the scheme is provided in Annexure – II.

(c) Yes. Impact assessment studies of the PM SVANidhi scheme had

been undertaken by Indian School of Business (ISB), Hyderabad in 2023

& 2025. The 2025 study was conducted covering more than 5,000

beneficiaries (with around 60% of the respondents being part of the

2023 study sample) and it found that the average annualized business

income among SVANidhi borrowers grew by around 20 percent,

between 2023 and 2025. Further the study also found that between 2023

and 2025, the adoption of digital payments among street vendors rose

considerably.

(d) The PM SVANidhi Scheme was initially implemented in statutory

urban areas. The eligibility was also extended to street vendors from

surrounding peri-urban areas who vend within the geographical limits

of Urban Local Bodies (ULBs). Under the restructured PM SVANidhi

Scheme, the coverage of the Scheme is expanded in a phased manner

beyond statutory urban areas to include census towns, peri urban areas

etc.
