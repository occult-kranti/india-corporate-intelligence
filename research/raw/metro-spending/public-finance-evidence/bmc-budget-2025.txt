          I am privileged to serve as the Commissioner of BMC
since March 2024 and I am delighted to present my first Budget
Estimates of the financial year 2025-26. Mumbai is not just a
City; it is an emotion, a vibrant ecosystem where millions of
dreams are born, nurtured and fulfilled. Mumbai represents
diversity, resilience and innovation that are the essence of our
Country.
      The general election of BMC is not held yet and there are
no elected representatives. Hence, we had made an innovative
endeavour of public participation by inviting citizen’s
suggestions on Budget Estimates since last two years. Last
year, we received 1,181 suggestions from Mumbaikars.
Similarly, we have continued this initiative and we got abundant
response i.e. 2,703 suggestions from Mumbaikars.
          This budget is more than a statement of accounts; it is a
vision for Mumbai's future.         It reflects our commitment to
innovation, inclusivity and sustainability while providing best
civic services and amenities to the citizens at bare minimum
cost and in real time.

Property Tax
      Implementation of final decision of Hon’ble Supreme
court regarding property tax is under process.       Hence, the
property tax bills for the F.Y. 2023-24 were issued on protective
basis on 26.02.2024. The due date being 25.05.2024, it was not
possible to make demands from the parties before 31.03.2024.
Therefore, actual property tax collection in F.Y. 2023-24
was ₹2860.60 crore against annual demand raised.         Further,
property tax bills of ₹6742.74 crore were issued on 13.09.2024
for the F.Y. 2024-25.    As such, property tax amounting to
₹5229.20 crore is received upto 31.12.2024 which includes an
amount of ₹1491.36 crore of F.Y. 2023-24.
      Considering this scenario, revenue from property tax for
the year 2024-25 is revised to ₹6200 crore which was estimated

     Capital Expenditure
      Historically, the revised estimates of capital expenditure
have always been lower than the budget estimates. However,
the situation is reversed for the F.Y. 2024-25 due to increased
expenditure on infrastructural projects.
      In the F.Y. 2023-24, actual capital expenditure of
₹18281.78 crore was incurred. For the F.Y. 2024-25, capital
expenditure was estimated at ₹36358.63 crore which is revised
to ₹37332.08 crore. The actual capital expenditure incurred
upto 31.12.2024 is ₹18991.65 crore. As such, actual capital
expenditure for 9 months of current financial year has already
exceeded actual capital expenditure for the entire F.Y. 2023-24.
This shows, increased spending on infrastructure facilities
while controlling revenue expenditure. Budget Estimates of
₹43162.23 crore is proposed on account of capital expenditure
for the F.Y. 2025-26.

     Internal Temporary Transfer (ITT)
      It was proposed to raise ₹6364.48 crore by way of ITT in
R.E. 2023-24. However, considering the total expenditure for
the year 2023-24, there was no need to raise funds through ITT.
But, considering the overall current financial position, it is
