                                               Ministry of Coal


       Ministry of Coal Opens Bids for 12th Round of
              Commercial Coal Blocks’ Auction

                                 Posted On: 16 JUN 2025 6:44PM by PIB Delhi



The Nominated Authority, Ministry of Coal today opened the bids for the 12th round of commercial coal
blocks’ auction (excluding 6 underground coal blocks). The round received good response from the bidders.
Bids have been received for 11 coal blocks out of 22 coal blocks.
The online bids were decrypted and opened electronically in the presence of the bidders. Subsequently, sealed
envelopes containing offline bid documents were also opened in the presence of bidders. Entire process was
displayed on the screen for the bidders. 41 bids have been received online as well as in physical form against
11 coal blocks.

A total of 38 bids were received against 8 coal blocks offered in the 12th round. Under 2nd Attempt of 11th
round, a total of 3 coal blocks were put up for auction and 3 bids were received against 3 coal blocks with
each blocks receiving 1 bid.
