## Checked passages and locators

Under **“Oxygen Plants Yet To Become Operational”**, the report states:

> “of the 75 PSA oxygen plants supplied under the PM CARES Fund and Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) initiatives, only 32 were functional by December 2023.”

Under **“Ventilators Found Unused”**, the SSKM hospital paragraph states:

> “five ventilators supplied under PM CARES were lying unused as of March 2022 because the required consumables were unavailable.”

The following auditee-response paragraph states:

> “the consumables supplied with the machines had been exhausted after prolonged use during the pandemic”

In the Raiganj hospital paragraph, the article attributes non-functionality to the High Dependency Unit not being operational. It reports 17 ICU ventilators received between 2020 and 2022. Those receipt years and the March 2022 / December 2023 observations must not be replaced by the article's July 2026 publication date.

## Permitted interpretation and boundaries

The article reports findings about **West Bengal public-health implementation**, including PM CARES-supplied equipment. It does not establish a CAG audit of the PM CARES trust's accounts, fund-wide misuse, corruption or a donor-to-recipient exchange. The 75-plant population explicitly combines PM CARES and CSR; the source does not support describing all 75 as PM CARES-funded.

The article's separate unspent amounts relate to Emergency Covid-19 Response Package-II and state Covid-19 untied funds. They cannot be relabelled as PM CARES balances. Hospital responses concerning exhausted consumables and a non-operational HDU accompany the equipment observations; neither response establishes the adequacy of implementation or its current state.

The retained graph relationship therefore remains a **reported implementation-context link**, with the mixed funding population, observation dates and hospital replies stated in its inspector. It is not a financial-transfer edge or proof that the CAG audited the fund itself. The original audit, its annexes and updated implementation replies are the appropriate next verification targets.
