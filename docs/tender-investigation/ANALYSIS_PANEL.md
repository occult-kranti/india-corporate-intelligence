# Tender investigation: independent analysis panel

Reviewed 6 October 2026. This is an agent-led methodological review, not consultation with human sector experts. It covers the existing `scripts/cppp` pipeline, retained aggregates and bounded schema discovery in the original SQLite files behind https://tender.sarthaksidhant.com/. The procurement engineer downloaded both databases and verified their published full SHA-256 hashes. No production records or claims were changed.

The site and Hugging Face mirrors represent the same scrape; agreement between them is not independent corroboration. The retained verification attempt found 40/40 stored detail URLs unavailable, so no sampled field was checked against the original portal. Publisher hashes establish file identity, not the accuracy or completeness of procurement records.

## Round one: testable questions and comparisons

| Panel perspective | Testable question and required fields | Comparison and innocent explanations |
|---|---|---|
| Data engineer | Are records duplicated, revised or joined many-to-many? Award fields already read: `internal_id`, `tender_id`, `portal_type`, `portal_year`, `organisation_name`, winner text, closing/AOC/contract dates, raw and parsed values/bid counts. Establish notice keys from the actual SQLite schema before joining. | Count distinct scopes and conflicting payloads within the current dedup key. Compare scoped keys and source versions. Lots, framework call-offs, amendments, reused IDs and repeated publication can all create multiple rows. A unique scrape row is not a unique contract. |
| Competition economist | Does single bidding remain unusual within comparable authority, year, category, procurement procedure and value band? Distinguish submitted, technically eligible and evaluated bid counts if the source offers them. | Use the same portal and full time window; publish all denominators and unknown categories. Require adequate counts and report intervals. OEM purchases, thin markets, framework suppliers, emergency exceptions and specialised work can lawfully concentrate awards. |
| Procurement specialist | Are notice windows unusually short, or award amounts materially different from comparable estimates? Requires actual publication, final closing, extension/corrigendum history, estimate, award value, currency, units and package scope. These notice fields are not in the retained award aggregate. | Compare effective windows after extensions, same procedure and applicable law on the event date. Check holidays, prequalification, re-tenders and exemptions. Compare like accounting stages, tax treatment and scope. AOC publication lag is not automatically evaluation time; a price difference is not automatically overcharging. |
| Civil/utility engineer | Are separately paid contracts overlapping physical work on the same asset? Requires asset/site ID, road chainage or authenticated location, BOQ items and quantities, lot/scope, execution dates, measurement books, completion tests and maintenance/warranty history. | Same title alone is a discovery lead. Check different stretches, phases, maintenance, disaster repair, cancelled awards and recoveries. Different assets with similar titles are a negative control; a repeated asset ID still requires scope and period checks. |
| Accountant | Can an award be followed to actual disbursement and delivery? Requires contract/work-order ID → invoice/measurement certificate → sanction/payment voucher/treasury or PFMS reference, recipient legal identity, amount, currency, payment date and reversals. | Keep estimates, awards, invoices, advances, retention, GST, deductions, refunds and final settlement separate. One-to-many instalments and joint ventures are ordinary. A concession can reverse the expected cash direction. Notice/award totals are not money paid. |
| Corporate/legal researcher | Does a verified contractor connect to a parent, director, donor or office-holder during the relevant dates? Requires statutory CIN/LLPIN or equivalent decisive public identity, dated filings, ownership perimeter, public office tenure and exact source records. | No name-only, address-proximity or shared-state joins. A parent is not its SPV. For bonds use validated prefix-plus-number purchase/redemption matching, not equal amounts/dates. Donation and award adjacency is not consideration. Require decision-specific records before inferring official involvement. |

The highest-value first pass is therefore a **notice–award trail with explicit missing payment/completion stages**, not a corruption ranking. Start with deterministic join quality and coverage, then inspect a balanced sample of flagged and unflagged cases. Do not select only the most politically prominent firms or the largest apparent anomalies.

## Actual notice/award fields now inspected

The full table schema is retained in [schema-counts.json](../../research/raw/tender-portal-audit/schema-counts.json). An independent read-only query of the first 100 detail rows in each database found the following JSON keys; [the key-only receipt](detail-key-sample.json) retains the exact queries. This is non-random schema discovery, not a field-completeness estimate. File identities are recorded in the procurement engineer's [award receipt](../../research/raw/tender-portal-audit/evidence/aoc_tenders.db.download-receipt.json) and [notice receipt](../../research/raw/tender-portal-audit/evidence/tenders_vps.db.download-receipt.json).

- Notice listing: `tender_id`, `reference_number`, `portal_type`, `organisation_name`, `title`, `e_published_date`, `bid_submission_closing_date`, `tender_opening_date`, `corrigendum_url`, `status`, `scraped_at`. Notice detail keys include separate **Tender Type**, **Tender Category**, **Product Category**, **Product Sub-Category**, **Location**, **Work Description**, **Tender Document**, **Bid Submission Start Date**, **Bid Submission End Date**, **ePublished Date**, and download dates. These support advertised-window and procedural/category comparisons after date/field validation. A `corrigendum_url` does not itself contain amendment history.
- Award listing: `tender_id`, `ref_no`, `portal_type`, `year`, `org_name`, `aoc_date`, `closing_date`, `title`, `detail_url`. Detail keys include **Contract Date**, **Contract Value**, **Number of bids received**, **Name of the selected bidder(s)**, **Tender Description**, **Tender Document**, and **Date of Completion/Completion Period in Days**. The last field is a mixed date/period label: it is not proof of physical completion. **Award Published Date** and **Published Date** appear as variants.
- No estimated contract value or payment key appeared in either bounded sample. **EMD** and **Tender Fee** are separate procurement amounts and must never substitute for an estimate or contract price. Notice **Name/Address** fields are contact information, not evidence of contractor ownership, a director or a political connection. No field supplies a verified corporate identity by itself.
- Internal IDs are stage-specific: use `internal_id` to join listing/detail within each database, not as a notice-to-award crosswalk. The procurement engineer reports only 41,825 state notices versus 2,916,702 state award rows, while central/`org` notices number 3,910,366 versus 2,005,258 central award rows. This grossly asymmetric capture makes a pooled award-conversion percentage misleading before scope/cardinality analysis. Counts describe records, not unique procurement opportunities.

## Verified findings about the retained analytical population

These are calculations over the previously retained JSON, not newly computed SQLite findings. Reproduce with `python3 docs/tender-investigation/aggregate-review.py`; [the output](aggregate-review.json) records complete input SHA-256 hashes and exact numerators/denominators.

1. **Changing portal composition alters the pooled trend.** In AOC-year 2015, 20,311/112,494 eligible rows have one bid (18.055%); in 2025, 28,767/406,391 do (7.079%). The central-portal share falls from 65.171% to 18.144%. At a fixed 50:50 portal mix, the corresponding rates are 16.403% and 8.686%. This is a sensitivity comparison; buyer, category, value and coverage differences remain uncontrolled, so neither series establishes a national trend or policy effect.
2. **Date classifications are not interchangeable.** 1,374,890/4,921,960 raw rows (27.934%) have a different `portal_year` and AOC year. State portal-year 2016 and 2017 each contain only 10 rows; eligible AOC-year counts differ. The retained rate family includes 175 central records labelled out-of-range year and 16 state records dated 2027. The original scrape predates those 2027 events. Timing SQL excludes negative gaps but does not exclude absurd/future years: the raw maximum AOC is year 9999. Recompute with verified original scrape cutoffs before interpreting timing means or seasonality.
3. **Missingness is associated with the screening signal.** Missing/implausible-value records are only 83,599/3,019,420 eligible rows (2.769%) but contain 52,498/338,909 single-bid observations (15.490%). Their single-bid rate is 62.797%; the complementary group is 9.756%, versus 11.224% overall. Other/unknown tender categories account for 9.683% of eligible rows and 30.917% of singles. Report these strata; do not silently drop inconvenient records or assume missingness means misconduct.
4. **The concentration minimum is applied to a different population.** Of 1,215 buyers meeting the existing threshold of 50 named, plausible-value awards, 350 have fewer than 50 marked-winner awards used in HHI, and 47 have fewer than 10. For 624 buyers, marked awards cover less than half of the all-named population. All seven maximum count-HHI cases are based on only one or two marked awards. Another 180 buyer HHIs differ by at least 2,500 points between value and count. Inspect units/outliers and subset coverage before ranking concentration.
5. **Short-gap observations are overwhelmingly same-day records.** 61,028/68,942 observations in the ≤2-day timing group (88.521%) are in its zero-day bin. Test exact timestamp equality, default times and elapsed hours before treating calendar-day differences as unusually fast evaluation. This aggregate cannot decide whether the source records automation, publication practice or an actual decision interval.

Five complete AOC calendar years, 2021–2025, yield 53,174/399,919 central single-bid rows (13.296%) and 98,399/1,351,416 state rows (7.281%). These are not an exact rolling five-year window and do not prove complete source coverage. The current extract cannot observe procurement after its June 2026 collection simply because analysis runs in October.

## Round two gate: controls for the newly acquired SQLite data

[Proposed SQL](proposed-controls.sql) uses the existing DuckDB `base`/`dedup` field names. It has **not** been run by this reviewer against the new SQLite files. The procurement engineer must adapt only after schema and source hashes are inspected. Preserve every query and its eligible population in the actual execution report.

- **Join cardinality before materialisation:** count notice and award multiplicities per candidate key; compute `sum(notice_n * award_n)` without creating the Cartesian join. Report zero/one/many matches and unmatched shares by portal, source year, event year and buyer. Enrich the key only with source-supported scope. Distinct `tender_id` alone is unsafe; generic organisation/year buckets are present. Keep conflicting versions, not an arbitrary “best” row.
- **Dedup falsifier:** count cross-portal, cross-buyer and payload conflicts under `(tender_id, normalised winner text, AOC time)`. Compare original versus scoped keys, and a conflict-quarantined family. Normalised winner text is a grouping string, not a verified corporate identity.
- **Coverage and survivorship control:** profile notice and award coverage separately; stratify by comparable matured closing-date cohorts. Do not call unmatched recent notices failed procurement: award publication is right-censored. Check cancelled, extended, re-tendered and not-yet-awarded cases before inferring a funnel loss. A lower match rate may reflect missing notices or nonpersistent source IDs.
- **Procedure/category control:** the old `tender_type` mixes Works/Goods/Services with Limited/Open labels. Preserve these as separate dimensions when source notice fields allow it; do not interpret the near-zero state Limited label rate as an open-competition rate. Verify each legal threshold, exception, jurisdiction and effective date separately.
- **Negative controls for repetition and price:** run the identical title/scope screen on different known assets, amended/cancelled cases, and legitimate framework call-offs. Compare award/estimate ratios only for source-matched scope, amount units and tax basis; retain raw strings and show value/count sensitivity. Never distribute one aggregate award value across a comma-separated winner list without evidence.
- **Political/event controls, only after verified joins:** run the same predeclared window against comparison firms/authorities and placebo dates; check pre-trends and event ordering, buyer seasonality and procurement-type mix. For graph motifs use degree-preserving comparisons. Report the search family and multiple-comparison adjustment when statistical testing is appropriate. Neither a statistically unusual association nor a short path proves influence.
- **Verification sample:** draw flagged and unflagged records within the same cohorts, include missingness strata, and retain retrieval failures as unknowns. Seek stable official IDs and archived source documents; do not bypass captcha or regenerate expired access tokens. Report source agreement only for fields actually opened and matched.

## Round two: executed raw-data review

The procurement engineer's [analyze.py](../../research/raw/tender-portal-audit/analyze.py) and [analysis.json](../../research/raw/tender-portal-audit/analysis.json) were independently reviewed. After its writer closed, this reviewer opened the derived DuckDB **read-only** and ran [round-two-check.py](round-two-check.py). [Results](round-two-check.json) retain exact SQL, both source-database hashes, DuckDB 1.5.6 and the reviewed analysis-script hash. A separate [raw-amount diagnostic](amount-lexemes.json) retains only public amount strings, never contractor/contact records. The earlier aggregate-only review remains distinguished from these newly executed checks.

**What the actual data establishes:**

- A `tender_id`-only join would produce **2,730,656,872 candidate pairs**. ID `1` alone accounts for 2,579,823,328 pairs across 207,448 notice rows and 12,436 award rows. These are potential join combinations, not contracts, payments or duplicate work.
- Exact portal + buyer + tender ID + reference reduces the match to **2,625 shared keys, 3,877 candidate pairs and 1,714 one-to-one keys**. The remaining 911 shared keys are one-notice-to-multiple-award cases; zero many-to-many keys does not make every match unique. Differences in scope and exact textual identifiers make low recall unsurprising. Neither low recall nor an unmatched notice establishes failed procurement.
- The old award dedup key really merges scopes: **22 keys cross portals (49 raw rows), 310 cross buyers (1,496 raw rows)**. Adding portal/buyer raises retained key groups from 3,385,233 to 3,385,618 (+385); adding reference produces 3,386,584 (+1,351 from the old rule). These counts confirm a bounded data-grain defect, not wrongdoing. Raw value/bid/closing conflicts also need retention and review; do not silently choose the first version.
- Every state notice is marked active. **39,104/41,825 have closing after listing capture**, leaving only 2,721 at/before capture. Of 46 state listed windows under 72 hours, 36 are future-closing and 10 are in the latter group. Central data instead mostly contains archived notices. Its under-72-hour counts include 82,068 archived/past-closing rows, 34 active/past-closing rows and 15 active/future-closing rows. This is planned or as-observed publication-to-scheduled-closing duration, not verified final advertising exposure, award conversion or evidence of a legal breach. Closing after capture is normal for an active notice.
- **The amount-parser concern is now specific, not hypothetical.** All nonblank award bid strings are unsigned integers; all nonblank award value strings are plain unsigned decimals. No stripped-text numeric change is observed in those two fields. But 14 central EMD strings and one fee string use scientific notation: `clean_num` changes `1e17` into `117` by deleting `e`, and corrupts the other exponent forms. Such enormous source values also require plausibility review even under correct numeric parsing. State EMD/fee examples instead use ordinary rupee-prefixed forms such as `₹ 0`; rejecting every nonplain lexeme would incorrectly discard legitimate formatting. Keep raw text, currency and scale; EMD/fee are never contract estimates or payments.
- Nonblank document presence overstates usable URL presence: **59,715 central document fields lack HTTP(S) URL syntax**. This does not prove the references are unusable—they may need interpretation—but they cannot be presented as fetched documents. Even valid HTTP syntax is not retrieval verification.
- A newly retained [field-semantics check](../../research/raw/tender-portal-audit/closing-field-semantics.json) explains why the first strict temporal candidate pool was empty: **1,709 of 1,714 exact listing-key pairs have award `closing_at` equal to notice publication**, while none share the notice closing timestamp or calendar day. None of the award times is midnight, so time-of-day truncation does not explain this result. The subset is **all central notices published December 2013–December 2018, including 1,632 Neyveli Lignite records**. It is highly selected, not a random corpus-wide error estimate. The historical gap between dataset date fields cannot be interpreted as evaluation speed without verified field semantics. Metadata labels are being corrected without pretending to recompute the old numbers.

**Concrete changes required before selecting or publishing review candidates:**

1. The procurement engineer accepted the need for strict lexical money/bid fields, raw-string preservation, explicit date-boundary tolerance and cache fingerprints. Existing `clean_num` counts must be labelled numeric extraction rather than validated monetary values. Changing a macro cannot silently reuse derived tables/results produced under the old recipe.
2. Require a unique exact candidate match; count excluded zero/one/many matches and blank-key records. Preserve unequal closing dates as unresolved version/amendment evidence; exact closing agreement still does not prove a common lot. Do not repeat one notice's window across multiple awards in a notice-level rate.
3. Validate event sequence and plausible years against each stage's actual capture. The detail capture ranges begin **16 June 2026 for notices and 18 June for awards**, earlier than the broad collection description retained in prior metadata. Publication/upload on 26 June is not a record's observation cutoff. Because timestamps are timezone-naive, separate same-day offsets from gaps greater than 24 hours before classifying chronology defects.
4. Sample short-listed-window + single-bid records and unflagged controls within the same buyer/category/year/value band, but call them **research leads**. Original notice, corrigendum, evaluation and contract documents must confirm scope, dates and interpretation. Do not relax the key or invent a control merely to produce a sample if the qualified pool is empty.

The procurement engineer subsequently added an explicit detail-reference fallback, canonical ID shape and finite discovery pool. Two 2025 IRCTC temporary onboard-catering records have listed 71-hour windows and one reported bid. This reviewer attempted their exact original ePublish URLs; both returned HTTP-200 **NoAuthorizationPage** responses, and Exa returned only a title. [Receipts and interpretation limits](primary-receipts/README.md) retain the exact IDs, hashes and failed verification. The records remain discovery leads, not allegations; contract/licence cash direction remains unknown. A 72-hour screen is not a legal threshold. Other source checks and matched controls are being reviewed separately by the procurement and services agents.

This completes the independent **methodology, executed-data and bounded source-access review**. Acceptance requires explicit residual missingness and date windows, linked source locators, alternative explanations, and payment/delivery stages displayed as missing until substantiated. Unavailable original records remain unavailable; no allegation or published graph connection follows automatically from these diagnostics.
