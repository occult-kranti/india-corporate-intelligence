# Delhi and Mumbai public-spending review

The release continues on `codex/education-funding-intelligence` after the flat-map release `5289489`. No skills are used. The investigation begins with Delhi and Mumbai and follows public institutions into national or statewide funding only where original records support the connection.

## Panel round one: research and presentation

Four parallel researchers own separate evidence slices: Delhi Police, Mumbai/Maharashtra police, defence/border procurement, and Delhi/Mumbai public finance. A fifth contributor implements city and topic navigation. An independent reviewer challenges source interpretation, current procedural status, identities, money direction, and geographic scope. The lead integrates the slices and verifies the complete published application.

The unit of publication is a sourced claim or funding record with exact identities and a dated accounting or procedural stage. A contract, allocation, payment, audit observation, disputed loss, and recovery are distinct objects. A tender notice does not establish an award or a payment. A ministry's Delhi headquarters does not locate its national spending in Delhi, and Maharashtra spending does not become Mumbai spending.

The first search covers public budgets and demands for grants, CAG and parliamentary follow-up, state and municipal audit reports, government resolutions, procurement portals, court records and attributed reporting. The tender index supplied by the user is a discovery source whose underlying notice and award populations must remain distinct. Records already present are checked for overlap; a refreshed outcome is not presented as an unrelated new case.

## Panel round two: challenge and integration

Before publication, the reviewer checks substantive claims against the opened originals and looks for subsequent replies, acquittals, reversals, recovery updates and corrected figures. An explicit response gap is visible when no response is retained. Missing evidence is not treated as an admission. Ordinary budget and contract records appear separately from allegations, findings and outcomes.

City scope uses explicit sourced locality identifiers, never the Maharashtra state tag alone. National and statewide context is visibly distinct and can be controlled independently. Exact identity cross-references are documentary navigation links with no financial amount. They do not silently merge companies, people, contracts, or their allegations.

## Acceptance

- Source archives have retrieval receipts, locators and hashes; source, identity and response references close without missing IDs.
- The new and retained cohorts remain separate. Counts distinguish cases, findings, outcomes, alleged relationships and ordinary funding context.
- City and topic scope, map/network switching, popup navigation, sources, exports, browser history, empty states and narrow screens are verified.
- Existing sector and evidence readers continue to work. The open-source discovery model operates on public authored summaries and never generates a factual relationship automatically.
- Push and deployment continue under the user's existing authorization. Public files and live browser workflows are verified after publication.

This is a bounded expansion with an explicit coverage record, not a claim to have inspected every government payment, tender, police unit or defence contract.
