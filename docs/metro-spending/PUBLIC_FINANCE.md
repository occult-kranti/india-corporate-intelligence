## Budget scope and arithmetic

BMC’s original 4 February 2025 speech has an explicit table for A/B/E/G/Tree Authority budgets. Proposed FY2025–26 receipts are ₹74,427.41 crore; proposed expenditure is **₹74,366.76 crore**, not the same number. Revenue expenditure ₹31,204.53 crore plus capital expenditure ₹43,162.23 crore equals ₹74,366.76 crore. The difference is ₹60.65 crore surplus. Internal temporary transfer ₹16,699.78 crore is internal financing. FY2024–25 BE expenditure ₹65,122.57 crore and RE expenditure ₹64,167.33 crore are not actuals. BEST’s undertaking budget is separate. None is a Maharashtra statewide or police budget.

The GNCTD speech proposes ₹100,000 crore for FY2025–26 and lists projected financing: tax ₹68,700 crore, non-tax ₹750 crore, small-savings borrowing ₹15,000 crore, Central Road Fund ₹1,000 crore, centrally sponsored schemes ₹4,128 crore and Union grants ₹7,348 crore, with remaining financing from opening balance. Borrowing is not a grant. The 72%/28% revenue/capital split is rounded and not converted into a falsely precise spending series. These are NCT-wide estimates; the geography has state DL and no city locality. It is not Delhi Police’s Union appropriation or an MCD/NDMC municipal total. No FY2026–27 or audited execution assertion is made.

Budget and procurement-context records are neutral. A common institution links them to historical cases for exploration; the graph does **not** claim a 2025 budget financed an alleged 2020 offence.
