Review date: 7 October 2026. Independent reviewer: metro_challenge. No skills were used. This review challenges source-backed additions; it does not generate allegations from similarity, geography or budgets.

## Round 1 — scope and source challenge

The review starts from the retained `research/raw/force` archive and the new four metro research slices. A city is a reading lens, not a claim that every ministry headquartered there spent its national budget there. Delhi Police belongs to the Union/MHA budget under Article 239AA; the GNCTD budget is a separate perimeter. Maharashtra Police, the Mumbai Police commissionerate, BMC, MMRDA and metropolitan-region bodies are also separate accounting perimeters.

The old force archive repeatedly says that city money “does not exist outside Delhi Police.” This is broader than the evidence supports. The defensible statement is that a separately verified city police expenditure total is not yet retained. A missing ledger or inaccessible portal must appear as a coverage gap, not a finding of concealment.

Adversarial checks required for every promoted item:

- Distinguish budget estimate, revised estimate, actual expenditure, administrative sanction, tender estimate, signed contract, payment, outstanding balance, questioned expenditure, agency-alleged proceeds and court-adjudicated loss. Do not sum stages of the same transaction.
- Mark the fiscal year and geographic/accounting perimeter on amount-bearing records. National CAPF or defence amounts stay national even when the source ministry is in Delhi or contractor headquarters is in Mumbai.
- A procurement discrepancy can support an audit observation; it does not establish a bribe, kickback, beneficial ownership or political influence without additional evidence.
- Preserve audit replies, vendor objections, acquittals, withdrawn claims, procedural reversals and later compliance. A rejection test is not evidence that the rejected batch was accepted or deployed.
- De-duplicate tender notices, corrigenda, procurement stages and cross-posted press releases by legal transaction identity. Separate similarly named individuals and companies; preserve aliases only when supported.
- Schematic city/state anchors must not become office, building, deployment or beneficiary coordinates. Do not publish sensitive deployment details or private beneficiary information.
- The new release is a sourced expansion, not complete coverage of every Delhi/Mumbai government tender or every police, border and defence case.
