# Investigation board implementation

Date: 15 October 2026. Status: implementation and verification in progress; no publication claim.

## Entry and retained product

The new research desk is `#/board`. The national atlas at `#/` and all original route patterns remain addressable. `#/investigate` opens the retained shared atlas workspace. All data is compiled from the existing source registry, with original tiers, source URLs, dates, accounting stages, rival explanations and limits. The redesign adds no factual allegation or inferred identity join.

The case board uses the existing browser-local Casebook, separate from the company Watchlist. A pin saved through the evidence workspace is available on the board, and a board pin is available in the existing Casebook. The board is not a separate copy of the evidence data.

## Features

- Source search across existing records, resolved and unresolved identities as recorded, and typed relationships. Counts are derived; result paging is explicit.
- Pin drag handles, arrow-key movement, Shift+arrow larger movement, and click-only movement controls in the inspector.
- Reading-order arrangement, board/list views and a restrained optional depth treatment.
- Original evidence tier line patterns, status/as-of date, source count and analyst-note indicator on pins.
- Source inspector with original source locators, limits, geographic attribution, financial stages, competing explanations, responses and a full evidence-workspace link.
- Four analyst-thread purposes: question, compare, counter-evidence to check and follow-up. They remain user-entered research annotations, not source assertions.
- A separately labeled sourced-link layer includes only existing registry relationships whose exact entity endpoints are pinned. No proximity or name-based joins are created. The connection ledger preserves the edge's evidence tier and status and opens its sources.
- Complete source-packet export with board layout. Additive restoration retains existing questions, notes and pin positions. Existing Casebook JSON/Markdown exports remain available.
- Browser-storage failures are visible. No remote account or synchronized storage is implied.
- Native modal dialogs with Escape, focus restoration and labeled controls. Reduced-motion preferences disable optional motion.
- Comprehensive route-aware help and source-bound tutorials are implemented in `src/tutorial`; the three standalone global apps receive their own local help.

## Cartography and meaning

The soft map underlay reuses the current 36-unit LGD/BharatMaps asset, preserving its linked provenance, all geographic components and stated territorial scope. Pins on the board are freely arranged research slips. Their positions do not encode geographic locations. Thread length, proximity, depth, card size and arrangement do not encode money, certainty or wrongdoing.

## Existing static apps

Global evidence, public-assets finance and media-power remain separate static documents with their existing model, filters, maps, graph and table modes, sources and coverage ledgers. Shared archive styling and navigation connect them to the India board. They do not claim shared Casebook pin/export functionality that they do not implement.

## Visual direction

The archive world uses stone/sage map material, ivory source slips, oxblood analyst wool, brass pins and a dark green reading cabinet. Depth distinguishes map, threads, paper and inspector planes; text is not perspectively tilted. Original evidence semantic colors and dash patterns are retained. Existing analytical/dossier views use the shared archive frame and their specialized controls.

The design review in this directory records published HCI, information design, art and ethical persuasion sources. It is an AI-assisted synthesis, not an assertion that named experts participated.
