# Oversight expansion: source-led audit trails

Research cutoff: **6 October 2026**. Sources retrieved on 7 October 2026. This is a purposive extension of a casebook, not a census of all Indian public funding or corruption. Audit findings, lawful transactions, respondent explanations and unresolved controls coexist in the same network.

The strict `InvestigationRawSlice` is `research/raw/atlas-expansion/oversight.json`: three investigation cases, three response records, 19 entities, 20 directed relationships and seven sources. Every relationship links its response record. No public official or private individual is accused through an inferred identity, a shared address, a name match or geographic proximity. No operational military location is included.

## Three added casefiles

### Uttarakhand reconstruction

`ov-uk-reconstruction` follows ADB → Government of India (sovereign borrower) → Uttarakhand's executing arrangement → project management/implementing units → explicitly bounded contract cohorts. CAG's chapter identifies both UEAP and UDRP; the ADB completion report only evaluates UEAP.

| Observation | Stage / population |
| --- | --- |
| USD 200 million | Original ADB sovereign loan commitment, signed 5 February 2014 |
| USD 168.34 million | Actual ADB financing/disbursement by financial account closure, 24 August 2018 |
| ₹1,141.43 crore / ₹1,319.03 crore | State releases to UEAP / UDRP PMUs through March 2018 |
| ₹1,013.20 crore / ₹1,164.54 crore | CAG's separately disclosed ADB / World Bank reimbursement to State; not equal to PMU release |
| ₹54.24 crore / ₹36.28 crore | Mobilisation advances in 64 UEAP / 56 UDRP contract cases, with bank guarantees but without purchase-invoice copies |
| ₹23.59 crore / ₹14.62 crore / ₹20.66 crore | DDMA release to Civil Unit (PWD), work expenditure to March 2017, and November 2016 utilisation-certificate face amount, respectively |

The central control question is whether signed contract conditions and supporting invoices reconcile these advances. CAG found that standard bidding requirements should have been incorporated; the department said contract data were silent and guarantees had been obtained. That is a documented disagreement over safeguards, not proof that the advances were stolen. The cohorts contain contract cases, not a claimed number of unique contractors. No unsupported private recipient is created.

ADB's January 2021 completion report rates UEAP **highly successful** and the borrower/executing agencies **highly satisfactory**. This is retained as meaningful counter-evidence about programme performance. It is not a CAG/PAC settlement of particular invoice or certificate findings. No such later settlement was retrieved. The November certificate and later actual expenditure are not netted into an embezzlement amount.

### Telangana police vehicle financing

`ov-ts-police-loan` follows State guarantee → TSPHCL → DGP-owned vehicles and State budget → TSPHCL → the Vijaya Bank facility (subsequently serviced by Bank of Baroda). A credit-facility node identifies the continuous financing relationship; it does not merge the historical banks into one legal corporation.

The ₹500 crore borrowing/guarantee, ₹328 crore budget resources for debt service, ₹185 crore principal repayment and ₹143 crore interest are overlapping measures of a single financing chain. CAG's ₹13 crore extra-interest estimate compares the 9% loan rate with State average borrowing of 8.17%; it is not another payment or the value of a lost vehicle fleet. CAG footnote 57 makes the comparator calculation explicit.

TSPHCL confirmed in November 2022 it borrowed for Government. Its own memorandum permits borrowing and government arrangements, supporting the lawful explanation. The unresolved audit issue is the project-revenue repayment clause versus actual budget-funded servicing and fiscal disclosure. No vehicle vendor, signed procurement contract, current outstanding principal, operational deployment or private bank account is inferred. Mirror CIN/charge hits were held: an open charge is not a current debt balance and the mirrors supplied inconsistent CIN classifications.

### Bihar PM-KISAN

`ov-bihar-pmkisan` preserves the division of responsibilities: Bihar validates eligibility; the Centre funds direct transfers; recovered amounts pass toward recovery administration. It does **not** create a fictitious Centre → State treasury → farmer payment route.

CAG's November 2021 validation population was **82,50,032 registered beneficiaries**. It identified 48,366 tax-payer recipients paid ₹39.05 crore and 19,485 other ineligible recipients paid ₹23.62 crore. The combined ₹62.67 crore recoverable estimate covers 67,851 recipients. These are separate from the field audit's 841 available records (out of 900 sampled) and 300 physical verifications. No national prevalence is extrapolated.

The Secretary's February 2022 statement of ₹5 crore recovered updates the approximately ₹4 crore statement of November 2021; the snapshots are not additive. CAG found reconciliation and remittance to Government of India incomplete. Recovery is labelled **self-reported**, with the official audit as the source of that attributed statement. Ineligibility alone does not establish intentional fraud by every recipient.

The State cited over 39 lakh rejected applications and accepted the absence of a State income-tax cross-check. The 18 March 2025 written Lok Sabha reply, retained as original PIB HTML, reports ₹416 crore recovered **nationally** and later mandatory land seeding, Aadhaar-based payment and eKYC controls. That national number does not clear or quantify repayment of the Bihar cohort. No later cohort-level reconciliation was retrieved.

## Provenance and limits

- `oversight-evidence/indexed-reports.json` retains opened official-origin CAG indexed text, with per-report extracted text and small excerpts. Telangana extraction hit the 300,000-character cap; the complete relevant Box 2.4 and footnotes are within it. It is not represented as the entire original PDF.
- Direct CAG original attempts returned HTTP 503. Retained failure bodies, response headers and receipts distinguish the failures from indexed reading. ADB's original returned HTTP 403; the official-origin indexed completion report was read instead. No access refusal was bypassed.
- PIB and TSPHCL originals returned HTTP 200 and their HTML bytes, headers, retrieval times and SHA-256 values are retained. No inaccessible PDF is falsely described as cached original bytes.
- The source hashes prove the retained artifact bytes, not the truth of the source. Publication date, financial period, response period and retrieval date remain separate.
- Precise public-site coordinates were not established; these cases contribute programme coverage, not fabricated facility markers. No sensitive personal or military data is collected.

The strict-slice/deep-case validator passed for this slice. Every mandatory next investigative step is a source request or a control/reconciliation test, not instructions for evading procurement, eligibility or financial rules. Final rendered review and cross-slice challenge decisions are logged in [PANEL_RESEARCH.md](PANEL_RESEARCH.md).

## Subsequent international-finance and defence review

The later expansion adds separately owned finance and defence slices; their cases are not new oversight-slice records. This reviewer independently checked their retained primary sources, with particular attention to sovereign borrowers versus implementing agencies and contractors, IFC convertible equity versus corporate debt, IMF reserve accounting versus programme borrowing, and proposed arms-sale estimates versus signed prices and actual delivery. The legacy finance and force fleets supplied discovery candidates, not automatic corroboration.

Material boundaries identified during review include the euro-denominated Bangladesh ADB loan, the single Tamil Nadu flood-system award to two named parties, the distinction between ₹600 crore IFC commitment and ₹300 crore received for convertible preference shares, and the dated Airbus delivery table. A recovered original Federal Register notice supports the MQ-9B notification estimate; a generic redirected DSCA index does not. Foreign-country context uses explicit country codes, without inventing Indian state coverage or mapping operational military deployments.

The additional decisions, source-access corrections and final-build browser results are recorded in [PANEL_RESEARCH.md](PANEL_RESEARCH.md).
