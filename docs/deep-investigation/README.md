# Follow the money

The new `/follow-the-money` page combines a geographic India index, linked relationship inspection, thirteen reviewed casefiles, whole-registry discovery, an official NSE securities register, and portable evidence packets. The existing sector pages remain available through the shared navigation.

The initial cases cover IRB and Adani Ganga concessions; L&T’s C4 railway contract; MEIL Polavaram advances; Air India’s sale and public cleanup; Mangaluru airport ownership and concession receipts; PEKB coal rights and related-party flows; Hindustan Zinc’s ownership and proposed transaction; Khyati PM-JAY claims; Byju’s financing and settlement proceedings; Akshaya Patra’s school-meal income and related parties; NSAP publicity expenditure; and SECI-related solar financing and legal outcomes.

Each case states the evidence, its accounting or legal stage, respondent position, alternative explanation, remaining uncertainty and the record needed to resolve it. Case inclusion is not a finding of misconduct. This is a researched sample and a discovery platform, not an assertion that every corporation, institution or village has been investigated.

## Optimised expansion order

Expansion is ordered by the evidence it can resolve, not by company prominence or a numerical suspicion score.

| Next workstream | Reusable starting point | Required result before publishing a new link |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Close the thirteen payment trails | Exact contracts, lenders, SPVs, CAG paragraphs and counterparties retained here | Signed instrument → certified bill or audited account → payer/recipient reconciliation, with period and stage preserved. Unknown use of proceeds stays unknown. |
| Resolve listed-company coverage | Original NSE CSV: 2,599 securities; 155 exact-ISIN joins; 101 held symbol candidates | Dated exchange corporate-action records and CIN/ISIN identity. A listing is not evidence of an award, political connection or wrongdoing. |
| Expand energy and natural resources | Coal allocation, resource, energy and tender registers already in the repository | Allocation instrument, concession or mining-development contract, ownership at the relevant date, audited flows and later court/action-taken outcome. Keep public rights separate from private service contracts. |
| Expand welfare and services geographically | Existing school, water/food, hospital, NGO, welfare and local-government records | Stable institution/LGD identifiers, programme denominator, budget and actual receipts, service outcomes, population/boundary comparability and attributed responses. No invented village coverage. |
| Reconcile public-to-private transfers | Air India, Mangaluru and Hindustan Zinc cases | Sale/concession distinction; public cash received, liabilities retained, future obligations, direct/effective ownership and actual subsequent performance. Do not label a write-off or retained liability a loan waiver. |
| Review private-company, NGO and unicorn networks | Audited reports, MCA/stock-exchange disclosures, FCRA/CSR disclosures, court records and existing registry | Exact legal entity and reporting perimeter, related-party counterparty and dated amount. Foreign funding and common trustees establish relations only to the extent stated by records. |
| Refresh proceedings and responses | Source URLs and procedural status dates in every case | Later bail, appeal, stay, discharge, dismissal, judgment or correction checked against the original instrument. Keep prior dated assertions addressable. |

Government procurement data from `tender.sarthaksidhant.com` remains a discovery source alongside official award records, original tenders and the existing reproducible corpus audit. Its duplicate identifiers and mixed date semantics are retained as data-quality limitations; they do not establish procurement corruption. No unlicensed private dataset, hidden personal record or unsupported inferred payment is required by this workflow.

The new reusable repository skill is [follow-the-money](../../.claude/skills/follow-the-money/SKILL.md). Open MiniLM inference is used for source discovery and is retained with model, corpus and result hashes. It does not generate allegations or identity joins.
