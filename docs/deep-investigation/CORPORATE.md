### Air India: compare like stages

The original 8 October 2021 award release gives two financial bids from seven expressions of interest: Talace ₹18,000 crore EV and an Ajay Singh-led consortium ₹15,100 crore EV; reserve value ₹12,906 crore. The bidder is not silently equated with SpiceJet Limited. Non-core assets valued ₹14,718 crore were excluded from the sale perimeter.

The 27 January 2022 closing announcement expressly says the government received ₹2,700 crore while ₹15,300 crore remained in Air India and AIXL. “Retained debt” here means retained **inside the companies sold**, not debt retained by the government.

The following-year Budget's actual column provides a firmer expenditure stage than the widely quoted revised estimate. Its ₹62,365.40 crore consists of ₹36,254 crore for past guaranteed borrowings, ₹12,357 crore for guaranteed sale-and-leaseback borrowings, and ₹13,754.40 crore for past dues/liabilities. This is not a buyer-specific bank write-off, legal debt waiver or proof that Talace received that sum. AIAHL creditor settlements, asset receipts and SPA schedules are still required for a complete net-cost calculation.

### Mangaluru: precise ownership and contractual price

The identity bridge uses the same CIN in the 2020 agreement and 2026 filing. The change of company name is not dated without a name-change certificate. The filing's 51/49 direct ownership split and 100% effective group control are both meaningful and must not be collapsed.

Article 3 grants a 50-year concession from COD, so no expiry date is invented from the signing date. Article 27's original fee is ₹115 for a domestic passenger and twice the domestic fee for an international passenger, using embarking and disembarking traffic and contractual revision rules. It is not the present fee, private profit, an airline ticket charge or a lump-sum asset sale. Article 27.1.2 states that concession fees are not aeronautical service costs eligible for pass-through.

The ₹11,200.27 crore non-current and ₹7,796.50 crore current AEL loan receivables from AAHL at 31 March 2026 provide a financing connection. They do not prove which airport used those funds. All six awards going to the highest bidder in the cohort is a concentration fact; rigging needs bid-file evidence.

### PEKB: contractual benefit and adverse findings survive qualification

The new parent-services link is explicit in the Supreme Court narrative and financial disclosures, rather than inferred from group ownership. No public coal ownership is transferred to the parent in this map. Likewise, 74% AEL ownership does not by subtraction prove who holds each remaining share at every date.

The 27 May 2019 Supreme Court decision is split: escalation survives as a plausible interpretation of the contract after clearance delays; the ₹78 crore fixed-cost award is set aside for lack of demonstrated loss; the claim to return mine-closure escrow fails. The judgment prints ₹837/MT in paragraph 2.2 and ₹895/MT in paragraph 11.1, so the slice does not invent a reconciled tariff or calculate a tariff-based total.

The 2025 road-transport proceedings are separate. Scroll reports over ₹1,400 crore of reimbursements, a ₹50 lakh trial-court fine and audit direction, and a later High Court stay. The ₹1,400 crore is not an independently authenticated final recovery award. The original trial/appellate orders and later disposition remain absent. The reporting sought AEL and RRVUNL responses, and that response status is retained.

The performance-guarantee memorandum has a handwritten **3 May 2016** date. Visual inspection corrected the misleading filename `04052016` and noisy OCR. Invocation directed is not money collected.

### Hindustan Zinc: investigation is not a verdict, proposal is not payment

The Supreme Court allowed the sale of the government's residual stake while directing a regular CBI case into the 2002 transaction. It did not reverse the completed sale or convict a buyer. DIPAM's reported objections to CBI factual premises are retained, as is the April 2022 reported statement that an FIR had been registered. No current 2026 outcome is invented.

The SOVL and Vedanta legal nodes remain distinct. The historical SOVL holdings and later Vedanta holding are separately sourced snapshots; a corporate-merger legal continuity edge was not manufactured.

For the distinct 2023 zinc proposal, Vedanta's original release argues for scale and shareholder value. HZL's reported reply says shareholder approval was required and no meeting notice had then been issued. The government objected to the cash related-party structure. Reuters' 2 May lapse report relies on two unnamed government sources; it is labelled reported rather than a primary board withdrawal. The original BSE copy of the government letter returned HTTP 403. That access failure is in the manifest and does not masquerade as a downloaded primary filing.

## Independent cross-review of the services slice

Reviewed the frozen `services.json`, its retained court/audit extracts, original Supreme Court PDF and annual-report page image. No substantive blocker found in the four case summaries reviewed.

- **Khyati:** the opened Indian Express report supports 3,578 PM-JAY claims, ₹16.64 crore reported earnings and the ₹8 crore loan-use allegation. The latter is not an additional loss or an identified bank edge. Bail and discharge are procedural; the case preserves defence and later bail rather than publishing only an earlier refusal. Direct court downloads and indexed court mirrors remain distinct in provenance.
- **Byju's:** the original 23 October 2024 Supreme Court judgment's paragraphs 86–88 expressly avoid a conduct finding and direct the ₹158 crore escrow transfer to the CoC escrow. The case correctly separates that rupee stock from the US$1,200 million facility and does not double the facility by adding its guarantee. The 2026 NCLAT dismissal is described as procedural, not approval of lender claims on the merits.
- **Akshaya Patra:** the annual report separately shows ₹42,093.64 lakh income from state governments, ₹36,788.98 lakh receipts and ₹1,351.66 lakh Chikkajala capex. Visual review of PDF page 80, printed page 149, confirms ₹275.76 **lakh** under “Rent and other expenses paid: ISKCON.” This is a disclosed related-party expense, not proof of diversion or an extra government grant.
- **NSAP:** CAG §5.5.1 supports ₹39.15 lakh and ₹2.44 crore sanctions, with rounded ₹2.83 crore expenditure charged to NSAP for other-scheme publicity. The amounts overlap. The source says payment occurred without confirmation of execution, which does not prove the campaign never ran or the money was stolen. The 2026 action-taken report is correctly distinguished from a confirmed resolution of this exact paragraph.

This review checks the particular amounts and legal qualifiers above; it does not certify every source, every personal allegation or later undiscovered proceeding.
