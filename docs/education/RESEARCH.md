# Education funding and access research

Snapshot: 6 October 2026. This release combines official education statistics, selected funding disclosures, NGO reports and private investor announcements. It is a source-backed starting collection, not an exhaustive inventory of every Indian school, college, NGO, funding opportunity, city or available document.

## What the collection establishes

- Government-school counts for all 28 states and 8 union territories across five academic years, plus all 75 Uttar Pradesh districts. These are reported institution stocks, not counts of individual closure events.
- Paired approved and released central funding for Uttar Pradesh for Samagra Shiksha and PM SHRI across FY 2023–24, 2024–25 and 2025–26. Six scheme-year records contain twelve distinct financial observations; none establishes district or school-level expenditure.
- Karnataka RTE funding sanctioned and reimbursed to eligible private unaided schools for FY 2021–22 through 2025–26. These are state totals, including Bengaluru, rather than city-level amounts or a school-by-school ledger.
- Official routes for broader school and college funding research: Samagra Shiksha/PAB documents, PM SHRI, PM-USHA, MERITE, MCA CSR disclosures and FCRA filings.
- Selected NGO, philanthropic and private education capital examples, with precise source periods and explicit limits on geography and financial interpretation.
- No matched city/catchment school-age population and school closure series has been established. No finding of unlawful closure, corruption or funding diversion is made.

## Research method and counts

The funding workstream made **6 Exa searches requesting 30 search-result slots**. That is a search workload measure, not thirty independently verified documents. It attempted **17 unique primary URLs**, received extracted content for **15**, and reviewed the relevant extracted sections supporting the registered claims. Large PDF extraction was capped at 16,000 characters in the initial fetches; the reports were not read cover to cover. The FC-4 sample-form fetch returned no content. The PAB landing page returned no Exa content and a fallback request returned an unpopulated JavaScript application shell. A separately fetched state PAB PDF supplies programme evidence; the empty landing page does not.

The public-sector workstream made **11 Exa searches requesting 76 search-result slots** and reviewed **23 unique URL text extracts**, including official sources, reporting and counterevidence. It independently downloaded the complete ten-page school-count parliamentary answer and three-page Uttar Pradesh funding answer for numerical reconciliation and page locators. Other long documents were reviewed in relevant extracted sections; the court record was not reviewed through its final disposition. The combined search workload is **17 calls requesting 106 slots**, not 106 verified sources. The two workstreams registered 38 distinct source URLs with extracted content at this snapshot. Search hits, successful page extractions, primary records and reporting articles remain separate counts. See [PUBLIC_EVIDENCE_AUDIT.md](PUBLIC_EVIDENCE_AUDIT.md) for document-specific verification and source-access limitations.

The repository's [source-retrieval](../../.claude/skills/source-retrieval/SKILL.md) and [evidence-tiering](../../.claude/skills/evidence-tiering/SKILL.md) methods were used for source access and claim review. Exa was used for discovery and primary-page extraction. Search-result snippets alone are not sufficient evidence. Government records establish what the institution reported; NGO and investor releases establish the named organisation's disclosures, not independent impact verification.

## All-state discovery, partial transaction coverage

The state selector covers all 36 current states and UTs. The official [Samagra Shiksha PAB index](https://dsel.education.gov.in/en/pab-minutes) supplies state/UT and financial-year document discovery, including supplementary minutes, addenda and revisions. [MCA CSR](https://www.mcacdm.nic.in/csr-data) supplies a national route to state-filtered corporate disclosures, [FCRA](https://fcraonline.nic.in/fc_dashboard.aspx) to association discovery, and education programme documents to government allocations. These national sources may remain visible when a local filter is applied, labelled as national context. They must not be counted as verified local funding records. PAB index rows also include organisations, so its row count is not a count of states covered.

State-wide government-school series are available across the entire selector. Funding amounts are a selected set with much thinner coverage. A local zero-result state means no matching verified record has been loaded; it does not mean there is no funding. Cities, districts, metropolitan regions, NGO registered offices and programme footprints remain distinct. Private-school, aided-school and college transaction coverage must be expanded through institution-level records rather than inferred from nationwide programme eligibility.

## Financial and statistical rules

1. Preserve amount, original currency/unit, fiscal or academic period and stage. Do not sum allocations, commitments, releases, receipts and expenditure into a single funding total.
2. Track included components and pass-through relationships. World Bank shares within a scheme, government grants within NGO income, and a corporate grant reported by its recipient are not new independent flows.
3. Do not turn an undisclosed amount into zero. Do not convert currencies without a dated exchange rate and declared basis.
4. Preserve school management and level. Government, aided, private unaided, schools, colleges and universities are different populations.
5. Institution-count decline is a change in reported stock. It may reflect closure, merger, opening, recoding or recognition changes. A zero-enrolment school is not automatically a closed school.
6. Compare closure/access signals only on aligned geography, boundary version, observation years and school-age bands. Total population growth and projections alone cannot establish that local school demand rose.
7. Investigation leads include alternative explanations and a falsifier. Reported legal disputes require case status and the underlying order before any legal conclusion.
8. Annual reports describe historical activity. They do not prove grants or scholarship applications are currently open.

## Panel review outcomes

Round 1 separated the six funding channels, financial stages and geography precision; rejected a blended funding total; and required visible original sources and coverage gaps. Round 2 challenged self-reported impact, inherited location assumptions and source dates. It removed a historical Pratham Mumbai claim from current city tagging and avoided treating Delhi-NCR platform presence as a Delhi allocation. Investor amounts remain undisclosed when the primary announcement does not state them. The final integration audit is tracked in [PANEL.md](PANEL.md).
