# Public works investigation roadmap

| Priority | Deliverable | Evidence required for completion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 — current extension | Roads/bridges and eight additional sector lenses; procurement coverage, audit dossiers, legal chronology and typed connection subgraphs. | Sources with original dates and locators, explicit denominators, responses, functional filters/export/history and two panel rounds. |
| 2 | Asset-to-contract ledger across maintenance and capital projects. | Stable asset/chainage and contract identifiers, scope/lot boundaries, award and variation orders, maintenance obligations, completion and payment certificates. |
| 3 | Reproducible current procurement refresh. | Legitimately retrievable official records, completeness measurements, cancellation/amendment links, source snapshots and repeatable deduplication. Expired links stay marked. |
| 4 | Corporate and public-office identity joins. | CIN/DIN or dated office identity, subsidiary-specific ownership, contemporaneous declarations and explicit joins. Shared names and addresses do not qualify. |
| 5 | Rule-to-contract applicability. | Gazette version, commencement, transitional provisions, procuring authority, procurement category, thresholds and the contract's governing documents. |
| 6 | Comparable outcome measures. | Distinct road condition/safety, service continuity, hospital claims, school access, recruitment and delivery evidence at matched locations and periods. |
| 7 | Reviewed refresh assistance. | Pinned model/corpus versions, extraction tests, retained provenance and independent review before suggestions enter the published evidence. |

Graph density, single bidding, repeat vendors, political donations and official
roles are not corruption scores. Local spending, party influence and physical
defects require their own documentary chain. Future monitoring must preserve
unknown values and competing explanations rather than turn missing coverage into
an apparent clean bill of health.
