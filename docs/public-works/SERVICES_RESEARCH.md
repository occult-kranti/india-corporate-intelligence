# Services procurement and payment research

Research snapshot: 6 October 2026. The recent **publication** window is 6 October 2021 through 6 October 2026, inclusive. Audit periods, award dates, response dates, and the present condition of an asset remain separate. This workstream covers electricity, water, hospitals and schools. It supplies 18 sources, 21 entities, 12 typed relationships, seven case dossiers and five legal/policy context records to the public-works evidence desk.

The workstream used the Exa Search plugin and the repository's source-retrieval, evidence-tiering and pattern-discipline skills. Thirteen successful searches requested 67 result slots; this is not 67 distinct fully read sources. Four schema-rejected attempts returned no results and are excluded. Eleven fetched-text receipts are archived, alongside the original electricity-rules PDF and its text. Existing education/water primary-source research was reused before searching again. The existing energy graph was inspected as a separate vetted corpus; identities were not joined to it by similar names.

## Panel research input and source review

The first research pass recommended separating service claims, school reimbursements, procurement awards, capital works, meter lifecycle service charges and foreign equity. It rejected a common shortcut: a repeated tender invitation does not establish repeated paid work. Comparison needs the same asset, scope, work order, measurement book, service period and payment identity.

## An aggregation trap inside the electricity audit

Paragraph 3.3.8 places the ₹26.65 crore headline before five grid-connection subcategories: ₹2.14 crore, ₹7.09 crore, ₹2.62 crore, ₹9.67 crore and ₹71.51 lakh. These add to ₹22.2351 crore. Paragraph 3.3.9 separately reports ₹4.41 crore of standalone-solar duplicates. Together they are ₹26.6451 crore, rounding to the headline ₹26.65 crore. The dataset therefore describes paragraphs 3.3.8–3.3.9 together and explicitly prevents adding the solar figure to the headline again. It does not silently rewrite the source's section placement.

The original matching keys also differ: some observations used account or meter numbers; others used names, fathers' names and addresses, and the solar sample included identifier mismatches disputed by management. The source-attributed audit result is retained, while an independent accusation against an identified contractor would require the underlying connection and payment ledger. No beneficiary IDs or medical records are published.

No graph motif is presented as statistically surprising. No null-model p-value, procurement quid pro quo, beneficial ownership, or political-control claim was inferred from these connections. ## Rules and policy versions

- **Electricity G.S.R. 437(E), 2023:** the original Gazette says commercial/industrial maximum demand **more than 10 kW**, while some government summaries say “10 kW and above.” The Gazette controls this record. Daily remote reading/access and time-of-day tariff provisions are separated from state tariff implementation. Rule 8A also requires ToD immediately after smart-meter installation for consumers with smart meters; the 2024/2025 deadlines do not displace that clause. The printed issue and notification say 14 June 2023, while the electronic identifier contains 15 June; commencement is publication in the Gazette, so the exact effective-day field remains null with the discrepancy visible. No consumer bill can be assessed without the relevant State Commission order and subsequent amendments.
- **AMISP DBFOOT guidance:** model procurement terms describe hardware, finance, integration, operation/maintenance and meter-month payments. A device-only quote is not a comparable price. A model document's version does not prove adoption by a specific DISCOM. Filename date is not assigned as publication or commencement.
- **AMRUT 2.0:** the 12 October 2021 Cabinet approval links city water-balance plans to city/state action plans. This is scheme design, not a blanket statute covering rural water tenders. A legal effective date is not invented from the approval announcement.
- **PMJAY age-70 expansion:** Cabinet approval on 11 September 2024 and launch on 29 October 2024 are separate. This changes the eligible claims denominator prospectively. The ₹12,850 crore launch-event headline spans many health projects and is not assigned to the coverage expansion.
- **Karnataka RTE 2018 amendment:** the 2026 parliamentary answer describes the neighbourhood-school exemption. The original state Gazette and exact effective day were not independently retrieved. It remains pre-window background with an explicit gap, not a claimed new nationwide law.

## Evidence and retrieval

The machine-readable handoff is [services-research.json](../../research/raw/public-works/services-research.json). Every finding has source IDs, attribution, response, alternative explanations and a falsifier. Monetary entries carry value, currency, unit, stage and period. Source publication dates remain null when unverified.

Evidence is in [research/raw/public-works/evidence/services](../../research/raw/public-works/evidence/services). `direct-fetch-manifest.json` retains original status/error, byte count and hash where available. `exa-receipts.json` retains actual search and fetched-text tool responses. Separate `services_fetch_*.txt` files make the decisive extracts reviewable; `sha256-manifest.json` hashes every evidence file except itself.

- Original AP regulator-hosted Gazette PDF: HTTP 200, 690,816 bytes, SHA-256 `27e2b78950aba1e43d0bdf6940ffd9acbf9afd6778ac2cb8f1420ca590ad7b26`.
- UP electricity chapter/full binary and AMISP guidance: HTTP 503; official indexed text retained. The chapter-only electricity extraction missed most body text, so the complete official report endpoint was fetched and read before using the findings.
- UP school chapter and PMJAY full binary: access refused with HTTP 403; no refusal bypass was attempted. An alternate full school report returned 503. Primary indexed chapter text contains the findings and replies used.
- Reused Karnataka RTE answer: original PDF and text already archived under `research/raw/education/evidence/rte-karnataka-2026.*` with its existing download manifest. Reused water survey, CAG text receipts and associated provenance remain under `research/raw/water/evidence`; existing raw education/water source records supply their dated audit trail.

Indexed primary text is not an original PDF archive, and a source's quoted official finding is not independent proof of every underlying transaction. No live site availability, current 2026 service condition, statewide misconduct prevalence, or exhaustive procurement coverage is claimed.
