# Public works research register

Snapshot: 6 October 2026. The register has 56 sources, 75 resolved entities,
58 directed relationships, 20 case files, 17 legal records, 14 named localities
and 156 unique historical buyer aggregates across nine sector lenses. All 36
states and union territories are selectable; the evidence is a curated register,
not a complete local procurement census. Sector lenses overlap.

The desk covers roads/bridges, electricity, water, hospitals, schools/colleges,
police, military, recruitment and administration. It links to the existing
energy, education, water and other registers. Private institutions appear only
where a specific retained record supports a funding, investment or regulatory case.
No private-sector procurement census or comprehensive local political network
is claimed. See the explicit expansion priorities in [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md).

## Evidence retained

Four reviewed JSON slices are assembled deterministically with existing CPPP
aggregates. The validator checks five exact archive manifests covering 57 unique
retained or reused artifacts (12,918,790 bytes), plus assembly input hashes.
Original PDFs, retained excerpts and retrieval receipts are distinguished; an
excerpt is not described as a complete downloaded report. Every source provides
publication-date status, observation period, retrieval status, locator and limitations.

The inherited CPPP scrape covers 2011–mid-2026, with 4,921,960 raw rows and
3,385,233 after its stated extract-deduplication rule. This extension reuses
committed aggregates: the 3.45 GB Arrow files were not downloaded or rescanned.
All 40 previously sampled stored portal URLs were unavailable. Agreement with
official records remains unknown. National buyer aggregates stay explicitly
national context; their names cannot establish local work sites.

Recent research uses the inherited 6 October 2021–6 October 2026 publication
window while retaining older laws, contract events and audit periods where
necessary. Publication, procurement event, office snapshot, effective date and
observation period are separate fields. Search and portal discovery links are
routes for extending coverage, not evidence that every linked result was read.
News and discussions are not substitutes for award, payment or asset records.

## Analysis controls

Repeated names and single bidding produce research questions. The supplied
aggregates lack the contract/asset/payment linkage needed to establish repeated
physical work. The interactive comparator accepts user-entered records and
requires exact comparable fields before presenting an overlap for review.
It never labels the result misconduct. Estimates, awards, payments, loan
guarantees, recovery claims and budget figures are not added together.

Graphs preserve exact legal identities, direction, dates and source limits.
A portfolio role is not evidence of involvement in an award. Consolidated
reporting groups are not their parent companies; disclosed party contributions
are not a finding of influence. Each case retains attribution, response,
alternative explanations and evidence that would close the question.

The actual local Apache-2.0 embedding run ranks 56 public summaries across six
queries for discovery. It creates no published claims or graph edges. Model,
asset and corpus provenance are in [MODELS.md](MODELS.md). Two panel rounds and
the newly authored procurement-investigation skill are recorded in
[PANEL.md](PANEL.md). Existing Exa/GitHub integrations were used; no unrelated
plugin was installed or represented as necessary.
