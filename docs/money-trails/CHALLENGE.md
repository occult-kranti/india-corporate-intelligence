# Money trails: independent investigative challenge

Review date: 7 October 2026. Baseline: `3d6ec88`. Reviewer: `trace_challenge`, a model-agent editorial perspective, not a human journalist or an audit opinion. No skills were used. This review tests what the evidence supports; it does not generate accusations from graph proximity.

## Round one: what must change

The existing `FollowTheMoney.tsx` already separates money stages, preserves attributed relationships, exposes responses and provides a source inspector. Its main weakness is not a shortage of caution labels. A connected component still does not answer how much of a particular payment is actually traced, what independently corroborates each hop, who had a documented decision role, and precisely where the evidence ends. More allegations or a larger graph do not solve that problem.

The new investigation should start with a falsifiable transaction question, show a bounded chain, test rival explanations, and publish the stopping point. An original judgment can establish what a court decided while its summary of a prosecutor's case remains an attributed assertion. Three articles repeating an agency release are one evidentiary origin, not three independent confirmations.

| Architecture objection | Required behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Graph paths mix money, ownership, office, source mentions and allegations. | Show distinct payment, contract, ownership, decision-role and attribution layers. Never convert a ministerial portfolio or common director into a payment hop. |
| The largest case amount is often the award, not a payment or loss. | Every amount states currency, unit, stage, period, entity perimeter and gross/net/tax basis when known. No pooled corruption total; no sum of award, receipt, alleged benefit and attached assets. |
| Documentary provenance is mistaken for the truth of every statement inside the document. | Distinguish transaction evidence, party assertion, audit finding, judicial holding, corporate disclosure and analyst question. Retain speaker and paragraph/page. |
| Name matches silently produce impressive networks. | Require exact legal identity or a clearly attributed case-scoped identity. A source mention is not a verified company. A group is not a single contracting entity. Show the unresolved identity and stop traversal there. |
| A minister's later or earlier tenure is applied to every event in a department. | Record office, start/end dates, relevant decision and source. Administrative responsibility is separate from personal intervention, ownership, benefit or misconduct. |
| A timeline uses judgment or press-release dates as payment dates. | Separate conduct/transaction interval, contract date, document date, publication date, retrieval date and status-as-of. Unknown transfer dates remain unknown. |
| The graph ends at the last named person and implies ultimate benefit. | Say whether the ultimate beneficiary is established, asserted or unresolved. A shareholder, director, agent or account signatory is not automatically the beneficial recipient of a particular payment. |
| New legal developments erase earlier counterevidence. | Append the newer decision and show its procedural scope. Attachment is not a conviction; bail is not acquittal; a charge sheet is not a finding. |
| Tender duplicates become repeated public expenditure. | Retain notice, amendment, lot, award, contract and implementation identifiers separately. Repeated work requires the same asset/chainage, scope, obligation period and paid bill, not similar titles or listing counts. |
| Missing documents imply concealment. | Name the searched source, coverage and access outcome. Unavailable bank/GST records remain missing, not zero or evidence of diversion. |

### Minimum evidence contract

Each trace should retain these fields, whether implemented as direct properties or typed linked records:

1. **Question and falsifier:** the specific proposition being tested, the strongest alternative explanation, the next discriminating document, its custodian, and acquisition status.
2. **Exact parties:** legal or case-scoped identifier, name as printed, role, identity evidence and effective dates. An unresolved identity cannot silently canonicalize to a similarly named company or politician.
3. **Each hop:** source/target, relation kind, evidence basis, attributed speaker, event interval, financial stage, amount scope, document/locator and limitations. A sourced allegation is drawable only with that attribution visible.
4. **Independent corroboration:** evidentiary origin, not number of URLs. Court quotation of the agency case, agency release and media repetition are not three independent transaction records.
5. **Arithmetic boundaries:** overlap/aggregate membership, outstanding balance versus period flow, gross/net/GST treatment, source rounding, and whether recipients of an aggregate are allocated or unallocated.
6. **Contradictory record:** response, later procedural or merits outcome, changed amount/status and the reason an earlier claim was narrowed or rejected. Export includes this closure.
7. **Stopping point:** what is established, what is merely asserted, what is not traced, and the next specific record needed. Report progress in documents reconciled and gaps closed, not an invented investigation-completeness percentage.

### Adversarial detection questions

This is a detection workflow: start from the records a proposed explanation would leave and search for them. It is not a guide to executing or hiding wrongdoing.

| Pattern to test | Records that discriminate between explanations | Important negative control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An award appears tailored to a bidder. | Successive specifications, published amendments, bidder questions, qualification sheets and rejection reasons, independent technical estimate, signed evaluation. | Genuine technical need, safety requirement, licensed technology or emergency timing may explain a narrow field. Compare the same procurement method and market. |
| Many related bids or rotating winners suggest non-independent competition. | Original bidder identifiers, dated ownership/control filings, full price schedules, evaluation documents and declared subcontractors. | Shared service providers, common templates, joint ventures and ordinary market concentration can produce similarities. Similar names alone do not establish common control. |
| Contract money appears to exit through a subcontractor or consultant. | Signed subcontract, invoices, work measurement/acceptance, GST treatment, certified payment record and recipient ledger for the same interval. | Legitimate specialist work, taxes, retention, mobilisation recovery and accrued expenses can explain margins and timing differences. |
| A public contractor also donates to political parties. | Exact donor-party instrument match, corporate identity, dated decision responsibility, award chronology and separately sourced communications or intervention. | Donations to rival parties and an award years later weaken simplistic timing narratives. Donation plus award is not evidence that the award financed the donation. |
| The same work appears paid twice. | Asset ID, chainage, package/lot, bill number, scope, maintenance window, original and reversal payments. | Duplicate web releases, re-tenders without award, separate stretches, renewals and amended contract values are not double payment. |
| Attached assets appear to be the destination of proceeds. | Attachment schedule, court's direct-versus-equivalent-value analysis, acquisition date, source of acquisition funds and final confiscation outcome. | Equivalent-value attachment can concern property not purchased with the alleged proceeds. Attachment value is not a recovered cash amount. |

### Executed data challenge

`node --test scripts/money-trails/challenge.test.mjs` currently passes **18 independent tests**. These combine synthetic traps with fixed assertions derived from the inspected source records; they do not use the product view helper as the source-meaning oracle. The suite covers contextual roles outside the payment path, source repetition without added money, exact release/transfer amounts and stages, excluded spouse ownership, unallocated political claims, conflicting execution-cost accounts, overlap arithmetic, pending-trial attachment, surviving bail counterevidence, sanction versus drawdown, aggregate sponsor contributions, direct-debit mechanism versus receipts, and the exact ten serial joins with an unresolved legal-company bridge.

An initial test exposed a real projection defect: explicitly authored role/ownership hops were included in the money-path graph because the filter excluded only gaps. The integrator corrected the helper to keep those relationships in the wider-context view. The regression now passes, with the minister/owner nodes absent from the synthetic cash graph and still present in its wider network. Two other corrections came from source review: a direct ED press-release allegation receives its own basis label rather than “in court record”; a party-denial citation must be classified as response/counterevidence rather than support for the allegation.

The browser challenge runner is `scripts/money-trails/challenge-browser.mjs`. It tests source meaning, exact amounts, identity/cash gaps, export limitations and the narrow-screen evidence reader against the served build. A passing syntax check is not browser acceptance; final browser results are recorded below only after execution.
