# Agriculture and food-chain research

Window: 6 October 2021–6 October 2026. Seven Exa queries requested 42 result slots; 17 unique official URLs returned text. Relevant sections were reviewed, not every page cover to cover. Older audit periods and cumulative programme totals remain explicitly dated. Undated portals are discovery context. The work follows the repository's [source retrieval](../../.claude/skills/source-retrieval/SKILL.md) and [evidence tiering](../../.claude/skills/evidence-tiering/SKILL.md) methods.

## Chain

- **Seed and planting material:** Trace batch, seed class, certification, region/season suitability and available quantity separately.
- **Inputs and risk support:** Separate fertilizer company subsidy payments, product availability, soil need and insured-farmer claims.
- **Water for cultivation:** Compare water availability and service outcomes; installed hectares or irrigation potential is not delivered water.
- **Crop and weather decisions:** Use crop/stage-specific local agromet bulletins with explicit issue/valid dates; weather workstream supplies the historic Cuttack example.
- **Area, production and yield:** Retain crop, season, geography, units and estimate vintage; do not sum overlapping crops.
- **Markets and price discovery:** Market arrivals/trades differ from production; preserve commodity variety, date, unit and reporting completeness.
- **Storage and post-harvest:** Capacity, usable capacity, stock, throughput and loss are different indicators.
- **Procurement to food distribution:** Separate procurement, allocation, state offtake, FPS distribution and household access.

## Documented audit issues

### Haryana transport-lifting penalties assessed but not imposed

CAG calculated ₹54.90 crore leviable transport penalties and reported no penalty imposed for delayed lifting.

**Response:** The Department said COVID-19 restrictions made procurement difficult and penalising transporters during the pandemic was inappropriate. CAG said no State Government penalty-waiver decision existed.

**Period:** RMS 2020 and 2021; response July 2024. **Locator:** Chapter 3 section 3.4.2, footnote 8.

**Checks:** Pandemic transport disruption and continuity-of-procurement priorities, as raised by the Department. Audited 2020 data covered only 14,159 vehicles because complete data was unavailable. Liability requires contract-by-contract attribution of delay; audit estimate should not be treated as every contract's adjudicated debt.

**Falsifier/update condition:** An authorized waiver, reconciled vehicle-level logs showing non-contractor delay, or audited subsequent penalty recovery would narrow or update the finding; preserve CAG's original period.

### FCI hired CAP storage while owned capacity was vacant

CAG assessed ₹62.76 crore avoidable expenditure associated with 10.55 lakh MT vacant owned CAP capacity alongside hired capacity in Punjab/Haryana.

**Response:** FCI management said capacity was hired expecting a bumper paddy crop; cancelling guaranteed-hiring tenders risked litigation. CAG rejected the explanation, citing continued owned-capacity underuse; no Ministry reply was received on this point.

**Period:** RMS 2020–21 and 2021–22; management reply March 2023. **Locator:** CAG Report 20 of 2023, section 3.4.3.

**Checks:** Advance demand uncertainty and guaranteed-hiring contractual commitments, as stated by management. Seasonal stock movement, suitability and location can make nominal vacant capacity imperfectly substitutable; establish depot-level evidence before extrapolating. December 2021 CAP phase-out instructions postdate part of the audited decisions and cannot alone establish their intent.

**Falsifier/update condition:** Depot-season stock and suitability records, binding contract dates, and documented post-audit action/recoveries could refine the avoidable-cost assessment; no current-status claim without them.

## Panel round 1

- Use an eight-step seed-to-food chain; every step links original evidence and preserves the stage's unit.
- All-state navigation is discovery coverage; distinguish actual numeric coverage and event-only village records.
- Expose period and estimate vintage next to every figure; never label old forecasts or cumulative counts as current.
- Show CAG findings beside management replies and unknown subsequent status; do not label assessed amounts as scams.
- No commodity-price/production or storage-capacity/stock substitutions; do not forecast famine from this collection.
- Use crop, stage, state, locality kind, date window and evidence type filters; local government programme eligibility must not fabricate local results.
