# Water-to-food evidence roadmap

| Priority | Deliverable | Completion evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 — this branch | A dated water/food evidence desk with all-state navigation, local discovery, source filters, supply-chain stages, observations and falsifiable investigation leads. | Traceable sources, explicit date/scope limits, working export/history/saved views and two panel rounds. |
| 2 | Village/city service continuity and quality records. | Stable JJM/local-body identifiers, administrative boundary crosswalks, reporting completeness, quantity/schedule measurements and the actual laboratory parameters tested. |
| 3 | Aligned water-stress monitoring. | Same-date catchment rainfall, relevant reservoir levels, aquifer measurements and demand; explicit forecast validity and local impact verification. |
| 4 | Water and irrigation procurement-to-delivery chain. | Tender/cancellation/re-tender identity, award and variation orders, payment records, independent completion evidence and functionality checks; bidder denominator and responses to audit observations. |
| 5 | Seed-to-harvest record. | Seed lot/certification, crop/variety, season, area, irrigation source, inputs, yield and actual losses; no parcel inference from state averages. |
| 6 | Harvest-to-household record. | Distinct production, mandi arrivals, price series, procurement, stocks, storage losses, distribution and household access, with units and geography reconciled. |
| 7 | Reviewed refresh and extraction workflow. | Versioned snapshots, source-issued times, stale/failed retrieval states, independent extraction checks and human decisions before promoting model suggestions. |

Local supply failure can occur during normal state rainfall; a meteorological
deficit does not alone establish a local drinking-water emergency. A reservoir's
percentage of capacity is different from its deviation from a seasonal average.
An audit finding can be valid for its sampled period without establishing the
present condition. These distinctions remain part of the product as coverage grows.

## Measured delivery cost

The final water JavaScript chunk is loaded lazily: about 467 kB uncompressed /
54 kB gzip, plus 3.4 kB gzip of page CSS. Model weights are never shipped to the
browser. The existing shared graph entry remains about 9.86 MB / 2.32 MB gzip;
profile and isolate that loading path while preserving cross-route research state
before considering a stack migration. See the [shared performance follow-up](../education/ROADMAP.md#existing-platform-performance-follow-up).
