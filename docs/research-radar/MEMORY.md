## Mission and durable decisions

Follow public money through exact institutions, legal entities, contracts, financing, ownership, decision roles, implementation and outcomes. Use an adversarial institutional perspective to ask what documentary traces an abuse would leave, then seek the strongest evidence against that explanation. Actors' incentives are hypotheses; an allegation or a connected graph does not establish intent, capability or guilt. Do not convert repetition of accusations into a personal prior probability.

The publication unit is a source-bound observation, an attributed allegation, an adjudicated outcome, or a preregistered institutional forecast. Keep those units separate. Preserve denials, acquittals, quashed proceedings, bail scope, corrective actions and alternative explanations alongside the original claim. A judge's participation in a case, ministerial portfolio, nationality or shared address cannot create a personal-benefit edge.

Numeric forecasts are currently **untrained and uncalibrated**. All new scenario probabilities are `null`. The implemented evaluation code can score independently supplied, timestamped institutional forecasts; its synthetic arithmetic tests are not model-performance evidence. Discovery embeddings rank text; they do not predict misconduct.

## Panel perspectives and documentary tests

These are AI research perspectives, not endorsements by human professionals.

| Perspective | Adversarial question | Records and rival explanation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Procurement authority | Did specifications or eligibility changes reduce meaningful competition? | Original notice, amendments, bidder questions, evaluation sheets and market baseline; technical necessity or genuine emergency may explain restrictions. |
| Supplier and subcontractor | Do invoiced goods/services, subcontracting and margins reconcile to delivery? | Legal identity, executed subcontract, invoices, acceptance and public tax/audit reconciliations; specialist work, retention and taxes can explain differences. |
| Treasury and lender | Does a commitment become a disbursement and an eligible end use? | Budget head, sanction, bank/audit advice, covenant and utilization record; financing delays and accounting classification matter. |
| Utility or welfare operator | Do service records reconcile with billed or subsidized beneficiaries? | Aggregate metering, entitlement, delivery and grievance-resolution data; migration, exclusions, errors and subsequent refunds must be tested. |
| Regulator and auditor | Were exceptions corrected, repeated, waived lawfully or still unresolved? | Applicable rule version, audit paragraph, department response, PAC action-taken report and later audit. |
| Public office holder | What exact power and dated decision does the source establish? | Portfolio and delegation dates, minutes and signed decisions; proximity and tenure alone do not establish intervention or benefit. |
| Court and legal reporter | What did the court decide, on which evidence and at which stage? | Original orders, procedural history, operative holding and subsequent appeal; distinguish allegations recited by a court from findings on merits. |
| Citizen/service recipient | Did the outcome improve, deteriorate or remain unmeasured? | Aggregated service quality, grievance closure and comparable local baselines; missing records are not observed failure. |
| Independent challenger | What observation would defeat the adverse explanation? | Prespecified falsifier, alternative mechanism, source independence and an explicit stopping point. |

## Person-specific research packet

Create a packet only for a public decision role or an individual materially named in a sourced case. Fields: stable identity; office/directorship with start/end dates; public financial interests where lawfully disclosed; exact decisions and authority; allegations with named speaker and case number; response; later legal outcome; outstanding source gap; provenance and knowledge date. Keep namesakes separate. A history packet never becomes a character score or an assumption that past accusations are true.

Assign separate extraction and challenge tasks for each packet. Avoid private addresses, account identifiers, family targeting, protected-characteristic inference and speculative associates. Where a public beneficial owner is supported, record the dated legal chain and its limits; ownership is not receipt of a particular payment.

## Acquisition and retention

Use official CAG/PAC, budget and treasury records, CPPP/GeM/state notices, court orders, regulator disclosures, stock-exchange/company filings, RBI/IBBI/insolvency records and treaty/development-bank documents. Independent reporting supplies leads and responses; copied coverage does not multiply corroboration. No contacts, RTI submissions or messages to third parties have been sent in this phase.

The user-provided tender archive was freshly inspected. Its index advertises a **26 June 2026** snapshot; that cannot supply July–October events. Claimed row counts include listing and detail tables and do not describe distinct contracts or public spending. Retain downloaded-byte checksums, portal IDs, lot/amendment/version and missing-data denominators before cohort analysis. Do not claim the entire archive was newly downloaded or analyzed in this release.

Each retrieval records URL, publisher, document/event/retrieval dates, access mode, source family, exact locator and limitations. Hash downloaded originals and separately label extracted text or mirrors. A hash verifies retained bytes, not authenticity or truth. Keep confidential/raw personal information outside public artifacts.
