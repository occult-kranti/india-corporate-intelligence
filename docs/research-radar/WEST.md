### Insider lens converted to records, not evasion instructions

The panel asks where an institution can claim success without reconciling end-use: utilization certificates versus bank/ledger stages; investment promises versus concession valuation; auction boundaries versus statutory approvals; quantity bills versus independently measured work; public land value versus documented service need; and capex announcements versus actual financing. Each case supplies legitimate alternatives and falsifiers. None predicts that an individual will offend or describes how to conceal a transaction.
