## Write-offs and recovery are different populations and stages

[Lok Sabha UQ 120, 20 July](https://sansad.in/getFile/lsapps/loksabhaquestions/annex/188/AU120_rCBUdx.pdf) asks about fifteen years of corporate loans. The response supplies twelve FY rows for domestic Large–Industry & Services instead, and marks FY 2025–26 provisional. Its ₹15,213 crore write-off and ₹4,456 crore recovery figures are annual flows, not a matched cohort. The separately reported 1,249 borrowers/₹4,19,380 crore snapshot covers all borrower types at the stated threshold. Do not turn this into a named-beneficiary or waived-debt graph.

The official-URL full-text extraction was inspected by the lead and independent challenger; direct PDF download returned 403. The retained extraction hash does not establish the original PDF's bytes. The response explains continuing liability after accounting write-off and a statutory borrower-data confidentiality boundary. Public insolvency orders and creditor disclosures are a lawful separate route for borrower-specific analysis. No confidential bank data was acquired or requested.

## Follow the actual FDI control rule

The [August 21 update](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2301992&lang=2&reg=48) reports 29 proposed investments involving ₹4,895.65 crore as ofAugust 20. It does not publish their investee identities or actual receipts.

The original [DPIIT PN 2](https://www.dpiit.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/03/b9da5830b052c2f2d788593e97d07c63.pdf) and [Cabinet announcement](https://www.pmindia.gov.in/en/news_updates/cabinet-approves-changes-in-guidelines-on-investments-from-countries-sharing-land-border-with-india/) are retained. The [Gazette](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2026/272196.pdf) was read through an official-URL full-text extraction after a 503 direct download. It distinguishes May 1 notification from May 2 publication/commencement, and includes cumulative direct/indirect rights, control over investor entities, ultimate effective control over investees, reporting and the multilateral-bank/fund exception. A nominal percentage or foreign jurisdiction alone cannot establish compliance or wrongdoing.

A superficially relevant RBI search result had a blank notification date and draft wording; it was rejected as operative law. No company-level ownership-evasion allegation was added. The prospective question asks for regime-specific proposal/realization reporting and exact public entity/control records before any adverse company-level conclusion.
