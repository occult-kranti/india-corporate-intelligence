Research cutoff: 2026-10-07, America/New_York. This review is an AI research perspective, not a professional or judicial endorsement.

## Round one: release conditions

The question is which **observable institutional or project outcome** merits another document request or a future test. A public allegation is not a propensity label. A minister's departmental responsibility, corporate connection, or history does not establish a personal payment, intent, or future crime. Scenarios must give the record that would defeat the concern as well as the record that might strengthen it.

The initial national collection is a selected investigative sample. Its source counts cannot estimate the prevalence of corruption, compare state integrity, or establish exhaustive coverage. Enforcement intensity, publication access, litigation and media attention affect who enters the sample. Unknown outcomes are unknown; missing records are acquisition gaps, not zeros or adverse findings.

Numerical forecasts remain disabled until the following conditions are met:

1. Pre-register a measurable outcome, event unit, population, geography, forecast horizon and eligibility rules. Use project or institutional events, not personal guilt.
2. Preserve event date, source publication date, actual retrieval date and earliest time each feature was available. A later judgment cannot become an earlier feature merely because it describes an earlier event.
3. Label positive, negative, unknown and right-censored cases separately. Track reporting delays and source availability; do not infer a negative label from silence.
4. Hold out later periods and disjoint legal-entity or procurement groups. Fit preprocessing and calibration only on training/validation data. Keep final test data untouched by feature or threshold selection.
5. Compare against time-appropriate base rates and a simple baseline on the same retained population. Report denominators, subgroup performance, uncertainty and class imbalance.
6. Assess probability quality with proper scores **and** reliability plots/bin counts. Brier loss combines calibration, discrimination and outcome uncertainty; a lower score alone does not establish calibration. No number from synthetic fixtures is product accuracy.
7. Version the dataset, definitions, trained model and evaluation. Monitor drift and revise or suspend output when source coverage changes. Preserve resolved forecasts instead of rewriting their original targets.

Graph review must distinguish contract commitments, sanctioned debt, actual drawals, public-office oversight, ownership and alleged payments. A graph path is not proof that the same rupee moved through every edge. No fuzzy cross-case identity merge is admitted. Duplicated reporting of one agency statement is one assertion family, not corroboration.
