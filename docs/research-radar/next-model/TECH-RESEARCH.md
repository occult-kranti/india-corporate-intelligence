# Technical research and local-model selection

Research date: **8 October 2026, America/New_York**. This is the technical member's evidence brief for an AI panel, not a claim that human outside experts reviewed the system. Sources and inspected locators are retained in [`tech-sources.json`](../../../research/research-radar/next-model/tech-sources.json). The recommendations below precede the new experiment; execution receipts and independent evaluation determine what actually ran and whether it should be promoted.

The strongest practical improvement is a better evidence pipeline: multilingual passage retrieval, explicit hard-negative supervision, lexical and semantic candidates, and a separately audited temporal graph. More epochs on the existing 64 English summaries cannot establish a model's ability to discover corruption or forecast national events. The old public test is exhausted for new blind evaluation.

## What changed in the literature

Eleven primary studies from 2024–2026 were inspected through original paper URLs, including current 2026 work. Relevant sections were read from provider text extractions; these sometimes stop before the complete paper. Model cards, architecture configurations, revision IDs and file metadata were also read directly from Hugging Face. This is a scoped evidence review, not an exhaustive search of every paper or a reproduction of their benchmarks.

The reviewed model cards/configurations for E5 and F2LLM use standard Transformers implementations; loading does not require enabling `trust_remote_code`. F2LLM's architectural 40,960-position limit is **not** a recommendation to train on 40k-token inputs. Begin with bounded 256–512-token passages, preserve sentence/source boundaries and required caveats, and record truncation. On right-padded F2LLM inputs, choose the last actual EOS position and verify that truncation retained/reattached EOS. Copying a last-token recipe without testing padding and EOS would train the wrong representation.

## Training changes justified by evidence

1. **Train retrieval, not belief.** Source passages and explicit query usefulness labels are appropriate. An allegation, minister name or high similarity must not become a binary criminality label. Store technical papers in a separate methods collection rather than polluting the investigation corpus with ML documentation.
2. **Expand by independent evidence families.** Page-level excerpts, award/approval/payment distinctions, correction records and disconfirming findings add useful supervision. Replicating paraphrases of the same 23 cases inflates row count without increasing independent evidence. Preserve publication/availability dates and canonical document hashes.
3. **Make negatives explicit.** A different date, a namesake entity, an administrative approval presented as a payment, or a contextual article insufficient for the question can be useful hard negatives. Unlabelled records should be masked when relevance is uncertain; each positive must include its rationale and evidence locator. Do not train against all absent graph edges as negative facts.
4. **Use small adapter capacity and dev-only selection.** Freeze the backbone, compare a finite declared family set, log objective/parameter counts/seeds/gradient steps, select checkpoint on development data, then invoke the new independent test once. The failed old adapter remains reproducible. A smaller training loss is not evidence of better retrieval.
5. **Retain sparse retrieval.** Contract numbers, dates, monetary amounts and legal identifiers benefit from lexical matching; use deterministic fusion with semantic candidates, then explicit reviewed reranking if implemented. Never let a lexical/semantic score overwrite source status or certainty.
6. **Challenge multilingual claims.** Include independently checked Hindi/regional-language information needs and OCR/numeric failures as labelled slices when suitable source text exists. Translated English questions alone do not establish performance on native-language records. Do not invent multilingual evaluation if those slices cannot be curated this release.

## Use the graph without inventing a financial trail

Keep three stores distinguishable: **observed source assertions**, **reviewed identity relations**, and **model-proposed research leads**. A passage-to-case association is useful for discovery; it is not an ownership, payment or political-control edge. A legally material name match requires an identifier or supporting document, date and exact source locator. Prefer a disconnected unresolved node over a plausible-looking but unsupported network.

Each financial edge should distinguish announced budget, administrative approval, tender estimate, award commitment, invoice, disbursement, recovery and audited loss. Store currency, amount basis, period, legal entity, transaction/programme ID where available, source availability time and event time. Aggregate only comparable fields; stock write-offs cannot be summed with annual recoveries to create a beneficiary balance. The inference layer should emit the **missing record needed to close a trail**, not an invented transfer.

Retrospective reconstruction is an evidence search with an **as-of information cutoff**. A later audit describing an earlier event must not be treated as information known before the audit. A forecast requires a specific dated institutional outcome, an eligible observed cohort, positive/negative resolution rules, censoring and a held-out time window. Unknown outcomes remain unknown. Evaluate against prevalence/recurrence baselines before fitting any temporal graph model; if no outcome cohort exists, retain null probabilities and produce ranked record-acquisition priorities.

For network discovery, conservative typed two- or three-hop expansion over cited edges is immediately useful and explainable. GraphRAG/Personalized PageRank can propose documents to inspect. Neither supplies the missing public records, and neither makes a graph path causal. Node popups should show every edge's provenance, status, date and uncertainty so readers can inspect the same chain the model used.

## Panel decision and scope

Round-one recommendation: compare a conservative multilingual encoder with a current compact 2026 family; improve explicit negatives, counterevidence and source-family splits before adding size. Round-two challenge: require the independent evaluator to see fixed rankings, test leakage and false joins, and distinguish retrieval usefulness from factual proof and future-outcome calibration. The proposed finite search is at most two new encoder families, with any within-family choice frozen before test evaluation.

The final experiment may reject this recommendation after measured runtime or development results. That decision and its reason belong in the execution/model card, not in a rewritten literature history. No model in this review is described as universally “best” or as a validated detector of corruption. The latest source discovered in this scoped technical search was from 2026; availability alone does not establish that its method will improve this application.
