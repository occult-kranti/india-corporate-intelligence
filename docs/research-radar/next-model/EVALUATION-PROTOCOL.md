# Next local investigative model: prospective evaluation contract

Authored **8 October 2026 (America/New_York)** by the independent evaluation AI agent before inspecting any new candidate outputs. This contract separates source retrieval, deterministic evidence-path inspection, retrospective reconstruction and future institutional forecasting. None is a personal-guilt model.

## Information barrier and new test

The previous 20-question MiniLM test is exhausted: its questions, labels and results are public. It may be retained as development or regression evidence, never described again as a blind final test. Do not modify its frozen artifacts.

The new evaluation reserves six institutional case families: Telangana police-housing vehicle borrowing; Bihar PM-KISAN eligibility/recovery; Akshaya Patra state support and ISKCON Bangalore transactions; NSAP–DAVP publicity; IFC–Mahindra Last Mile Mobility; and IBRD loan 8488-IN/Tamil Nadu flood-monitoring procurement. All their source summaries, cross-namespace duplicates, source URLs and material entity neighbors are excluded from gradient updates and development checkpoint selection. Broad oversight institutions (CAG, World Bank, Government of India) alone do not join every unrelated case. The split manifest records exact exclusions and semantic aliases; it does not claim all private or unknown affiliations were found.

The independent evaluator authors **24 questions, four per case family**, using retained source records and available original/indexed passages, without candidate rankings. Query labels grade source relevance (2 direct; 1 useful but incomplete; 0 not an answer to that question). Zero is not a false source, innocent actor or absent real-world connection. Exact historical period and financial stage are part of relevance. At test time the held-out source summaries are legitimate index candidates: this is closed-corpus retrieval, not a cold-document deployment test. Upstream pretraining exposure is unknown.

The candidate unit is a canonical source-summary document containing its retained title, publisher, locator, summary and limitations. Typed graph edges are attached through canonical IDs **after retrieval** and do not enter supervised training as evidence of wrongdoing. Model text excludes query labels, anticipated answers and separately authored hypothetical outcomes. Train and development files, corpus projection, model configurations, code and these independent labels receive SHA-256 hashes before final invocation. Any pre-output correction must have a recorded reason and version; post-output label changes invalidate the prospective result.

## Finite training and selection budget

At most **two new pretrained encoder families** may be profiled. Hardware/tokenization smoke checks and training-only loss checks may repair implementation faults; they do not authorize final-test inspection. Record the exact upstream revision, license, pooling/prefix convention, token cap, numeric precision, trainable parameter count, optimizer, learning-rate schedule, seeds, batch sizes, epoch limit and elapsed time. Freeze the actual experiment grid before the first development ranking. Allow at most **four candidate configurations in total**, each with at most **eight epochs**, and select one checkpoint using development case-macro nDCG@5; counterevidence recall breaks a tie, then fewer trainable parameters/earlier epoch. A stop caused by CPU time or memory is disclosed, not silently omitted.

Compare the single development-selected final candidate against BM25 and its own untuned encoder on exactly the same corpus, questions, tokenization and retrieval conditions. The existing MiniLM default provides a continuity comparator where runnable. A frozen lexical/neural fusion may be selected on development data, but its weight counts as one of the four configurations. Do not inspect final-test rankings to choose model family, epoch, negative sampler, fusion weight, tokenizer, threshold or prompt. After final evaluation, the new test is also exhausted for future tuning.

## Retrieval metrics and release gate

Every comparator ranks each eligible source ID once with deterministic source-ID tie breaking; retain raw scores and full rankings. Report query mean, per-family results and equal-weight case-family macro mean for:

- nDCG@5 (gain 2^grade − 1), MRR@10 and Recall@5.
- Required counterevidence Recall@5, denominator only questions with required counterevidence.
- Source-lineage-collapsed nDCG@5/Recall@5, retaining the first hit per lineage. A repeated story is not an independent confirmation; distinct families are not necessarily independent causal evidence.
- Explicit wrong-period/wrong-money-stage hard-negative preference below the first positive; absent graph edges are not sampled as factual negatives.

Use a paired exploratory bootstrap over the **six whole case families**, fixed seed 20261008, 5,000 resamples, for candidate-minus-own-base nDCG@5. These selected agent-authored labels are not representative national users or independently sampled corruption cases; six groups do not support broad accuracy claims.

Default retrieval promotion requires all of: case-macro nDCG@5 at least **0.02 above its own untuned base**, no more than **0.01 below BM25**, required-counterevidence Recall@5 and source Recall@5 no more than **0.01 below either base or BM25**, at least **four of six groups improve** over its base, and no group loses more than **0.05** nDCG@5. If the continuity MiniLM comparator is available, nDCG@5 must also be no more than **0.01 below it**. Missing or unmeasurable conditions fail promotion. A failed candidate can be retained experimentally; no final-test-driven second attempt is permitted. A faster/smaller model may be selected explicitly as a resource tradeoff, but not described as passing this quality gate.

## Evidence-path and abstention checks

For the 24 questions, retain required/acceptable source IDs, typed edge IDs, money stage, case-local limitations, counterevidence, and explicit unsupported continuations. A separate deterministic integration check verifies that every displayed path edge exists in the frozen registry, retains original direction/type/status/source IDs, and cannot turn a guarantee, approval, ownership link, contract award or national recovery total into cash paid to a named beneficiary. Graph expansion must not merge entities by name similarity, infer wrongdoing from proximity or manufacture a missing endpoint. Source closure recall and exact path-edge preservation are reported separately from learned retrieval metrics; a successful parser is not a learned reasoning score.

At least one question per family requires an evidence stop: missing vehicle supplier; missing cohort remittance; no restricted-grant trace to a related-party rent payment; no named private advertising beneficiary; no verified later investment tranche; or no contractor cash/per-firm award split. Missing evidence means **unknown/not established in this corpus**, not false and not zero. These editorial expectations assess whether the interface carries the limit forward; similarity scores never become probability or factual confidence. If no generative model is trained, report structured evidence retrieval and rule enforcement, not natural-language prediction accuracy.

## Temporal reconstruction and genuine forecasting

The current tender archive is a June 2026 snapshot. Contract dates and publication fields inside a later scrape do not demonstrate those features were knowable at an earlier prediction time. Award winners, bidder counts, eventual payment, project status, retrospective audit language and subsequent press coverage are outcome/leakage fields when not demonstrably available at the cutoff. The World Bank current-project snapshot has the same issue. A date-sorted split alone cannot repair post-event source availability.

Any exercise masking an already recorded field must be named **retrospective masked-record reconstruction**, with observed-record selection, missingness and retrieval leakage disclosed. No absent award is coded as failure and no unresolved edge as a negative. Train/test case/entity/lineage grouping and temporal availability checks are separate requirements.

Numeric institutional forecasts require a preregistered measurable event, exact cutoff/deadline, representative eligible cohort, feature-availability timestamps, resolution rule/source and independently adjudicated outcomes. Evaluate Brier/log loss and reliability against same-question reference-class and persistence baselines, keeping unknown/censored outcomes separate. Existing selected unresolved scenarios lack that cohort; therefore probabilities and forecast skill stay **unestimated**. New hypotheses may name observable documentary implications and alternative explanations but never predict an individual's criminal propensity.

## Publication and reproducibility

Keep a machine-readable receipt of split, labels, configurations, model weights, executable code, raw rankings, metric outputs and timestamps. The final report must state actual executed training versus planned experiments, source-summary versus full-document scope, agent-authored labels, observed exclusions, failures and corpus limitations. Neither training more parameters nor winning one editorial benchmark proves unseen corruption, exhaustive money trails or future-event calibration.
