# Investigation roadmaps

The time window is 26 May 2014–14 October 2026, with explicitly dated earlier context. These are work plans; unperformed acquisitions are not evidence. The full sector continuation register is `research/hypothesis-lab/coverage.json` and is displayed on the new page.

## 1. Establish each decision and its alternatives

For the selected political-finance, resources/contracts and oversight/services files, retain the original legal or administrative rule, the actor's actual authority, the decision date, subsequent evidence and the strongest contrary finding. Separate structural incentives from assertions about private intent. Exit criterion: every hypothesis has a lawful rival, an error rival, at least two discriminating tests and a dated comparison design.

## 2. Obtain missing financial and coordination records

Start with the named document holders in each case. Link exact legal entities, operative dates, contracts, lots, notices and amendments before looking for payments. Keep approval, award, invoice, payment, receipt, recovery, write-off and waiver distinct. A link is promoted only when a source establishes its endpoints, relation, period and stage; retain the previous question and promotion reason. No automated contacting, RTI filing or external messages were performed.

## 3. Test common causes and comparison groups

Record the population and selection rule before computing new results. Compare similarly eligible suppliers, procedures, periods, asset types and jurisdictions. Separate nonparticipation from bid rejection and a failed auction from a negotiated award. Compare acquisition completeness as well as outcomes. Publish null and contradictory results. Preserve correlated source families rather than counting several articles about one order as several independent confirmations.

## 4. Extend beyond selected files

The twenty-sector register links to the existing atlas and states which new hypotheses have not been reviewed. Prioritize by document availability and the ability to discriminate between explanations, not by political salience or a score implying guilt. Defence offsets need acceptance/recovery ledgers; water needs onward payments and service verification; welfare needs beneficiary-period reconciliation; PM CARES needs agency-level refund notes; education needs stable school/geography/age denominators; finance needs current legal dispositions and recovery records. These are acquisition priorities, not findings of wrongdoing.

## 5. Evaluate prospective institutional outcomes

Keep current retrospective case tests separate from future forecasts. For a new prospective cohort, freeze the population, source versions, features, prediction timestamp, outcome definition and missingness rule before the outcomes arrive. Forecast observable record changes or contract milestones, not secret culpability. Use an untouched evaluation set, temporal/entity/source-family separation, simple baselines and calibration measurements. The existing World Bank April 2027 observation plan remains unchanged. No scheduler, new model training or predictive performance is claimed here.

## 6. Review and publish

An independent agent challenges the strongest adverse assertion, the legal version, the source join and the effect of contrary evidence. The design panel checks whether a reader can see a question, inspect a node, open its source, compare explanations and export a closed packet on desktop and mobile. Run the deterministic bundle/hash checks, malformed-data tests, browser workflows and required deployment gates. Preserve prior test failures and limits instead of changing acceptance criteria to make a report green.
