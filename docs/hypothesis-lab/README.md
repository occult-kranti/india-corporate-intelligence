# Coordination hypotheses

Route: `/#/hypothesis-lab`. Branch: `codex/education-funding-intelligence`.

This page adds a source-led investigation of selected institutional decisions during the Modi Union administrations, 26 May 2014–14 October 2026, with earlier context explicitly dated. It does not claim discovery of secret conspiracies, complete government coverage or calibrated predictive performance.

The reviewed bundle contains **8 files, 32 source entries (32 URLs, 31 declared source families), 61 scoped identities, 54 recorded relationships, 11 unestablished link questions and 19 discriminating tests**. Eighteen rejected or unestablished joins are preserved. Four source entries explicitly rely on secondary reporting; each retains its inspection scope and limits. All source entries have retained captures and SHA-256 receipts, including clearly identified extracts and mirrored documents. A capture is not independent confirmation of the source's assertions.

## Interface and evidence contract

The flat India map and relationship network share clickable evidence readers. Search and filters select a file; URL state preserves the selection, view, source and question layer. The optional **Show questions to test** layer starts off. Its dashed analytic edges have no amounts and cannot complete recorded money trails. Unresolved counterparties remain in the text and packet without invented map identities.

The reader separates the proposition, current assessment, known facts, missing facts, three rival explanations, tests, institutional decision options, operative rules, chronology, recorded stages, next documents and contrary evidence. Each source reader exposes its locator, inspection depth, retrieval time, family, capture and limitations. Case downloads close all nested sources and keep original recorded edges separate from proposed links. Shared graph exports explicitly partition mixed evidence/question displays and preserve their full closure.

The twenty-sector continuation register links to the existing application and distinguishes selected reviews from inherited records and planned acquisition. It does not silently promote earlier atlas records into newly reviewed claims. No fuzzy cross-stream entity merge, pooled money total, guilt score, automatic external outreach or new model training is added.
