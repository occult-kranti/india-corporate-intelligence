## Decision reconstruction and game theory

A decision worksheet begins with a dated institutional action, not a named person's presumed malign character. For each case it can store:

1. **Actor and authority:** exact legal entity/public role, signing power and applicable rule at the decision date. A ministerial role does not establish personal approval of every contract.
2. **Information then:** contemporaneously available bid, audit, budget or warning records. Distinguish the institution's actual receipt from later public availability; either can be unknown. Later reporting belongs in hindsight context.
3. **Constraints and feasible options:** budget authority, competitive procedure, emergency exception, technical supply limits, court orders, deadlines and documented options. Do not invent an exhaustive option set.
4. **Qualitative incentive hypotheses:** timely delivery, cost control, vendor capacity, electoral visibility or an adverse conflict hypothesis. Each adverse hypothesis needs evidence and an ordinary alternative. Unknown preferences remain unknown.
5. **Observable consequences:** an executed amendment, award, payment, delivery, court disposition or public reporting obligation. Define the source that would confirm or contradict each option's expected result.
6. **Observed outcome and falsifier:** separate the recorded outcome from its possible causes; specify the next document that could eliminate a preferred explanation.

`decisionAnalysis` is optional per case. Its actors, options and outcome cite exact registered sources; `hindsightLimits` is mandatory if used. Its schema does not authorize a “guilt payoff”, psychological assessment, equilibrium estimate or prediction of personal misconduct. Backward reasoning asks which records should exist under rival explanations; it does not recover unobserved intent merely because one explanation fits the result. With unknown preferences and incomplete information, several strategies can explain the same award.

For attack or false-flag assertions, spending, later beneficiaries, security lapses and political advantage do not identify an attacker or establish staging. Public inquiry findings, authenticated incident records, judicial dispositions and attributed statements have distinct evidential force. Treat procurement accountability and attack attribution as separate questions; an unsupported attribution stays rejected or unresolved, without an invented connection.

## Money and identity rules

An admissible transaction needs exact parties, financial stage, amount/unit/currency, date or period, instrument identifier where available, a source locator and limitations. Budget estimates, revised estimates, actual expenditure, contract face value, an allocation, an attachment, a recovery and an agency's alleged transfer are different quantities. A bank write-off is not automatically a waiver; a booked loss is not automatically a cash transfer. An award and its disbursement cannot be added as two distinct losses. Fund interest and corpus receipts must not be counted again as fresh external contributions.

Entity joins require an authoritative legal identifier or a documented identity crosswalk with time validity. Name similarity, common addresses, shared intermediaries, membership in a group, and network centrality are acquisition leads. An existing identity crosswalk may be navigated only as labelled context, never as another transfer. New track namespaces intentionally remain separate until reviewed evidence supports a merge. Repeated press releases and mirrored court copies form one underlying source family, not several confirmations.

Unknown values remain null. Missing originals, inaccessible pages, partial reading, selected sources and ordinary institutional explanations remain visible in the UI and exports. A mechanically valid citation cannot prove that the cited passage supports an assertion; the second panel must still read and challenge it.

## Executed local tools

```bash
python3 scripts/funding-investigations/validate.py \
  --output research/funding-investigations/analysis-validation.json
python3 scripts/funding-investigations/validate.test.py
python3 scripts/funding-investigations/trace.test.py
python3 scripts/funding-investigations/trace.py \
  --query 'water payment contractor audit response' \
  --output research/funding-investigations/analysis-water-search.json
python3 scripts/funding-investigations/trace.py \
  --from-id 'EXACT_ENTITY_ID' --to-id 'EXACT_ENTITY_ID' --depth 3 \
  --output research/funding-investigations/analysis-path.json
```

`trace.py` imports the current registry through its existing loader; `--registry FILE` permits a preserved JSON snapshot. It reads new stream files and validates them before use. Search is deterministic BM25 over source/entity/record text, with no stochastic model or new training. It may surface an irrelevant or outdated legacy record: the result includes corpus origin, source references and exact IDs and must be inspected. No semantic cross-track merge is created.

Navigation defaults to excluding context/crosswalk edges; `--include-context` admits them with `contextOnly: true`. Paths preserve original arrows even when traversed backwards for exploration. Depth is capped at five, output path caps and truncation are explicit, and all traversed original assertion objects and source records are exported. A path is documentary adjacency, not a continuous cash chain or validated historical information set. Authored missing-record questions attach only to their exact existing edges. No graph money total, inferred edge, probability or personal score is produced.

The validator checks ID uniqueness, required fields, exact references, local capture containment and SHA-256, timezone-aware access timestamps, source reading scope, amount-stage metadata, and alternatives/falsifiers. Adversarial fixtures include source tampering, path escape/symlinks, illegal stages, missing endpoints, NaN/infinite/bool amounts, hidden aggregate/guilt fields and broken decision references. Passing these checks does not certify source authenticity, every legal interpretation, quotation accuracy or arithmetic reconciliation; those require passage review.
