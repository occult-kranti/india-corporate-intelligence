# Research lead memory

Updated for the 8 October 2026 review. This is project memory, not evidence about a subject's motives.

## Keep these decisions

The user's aim is a fifteen-year, cross-sector account of institutional decisions, authority, money and outcomes. Start with the existing sector networks and exact records. Use specialist acquisition in parallel, then a different specialist to challenge the strongest adverse assertion. The team members are AI research roles, not independent witnesses or human experts.

Reconstruct each decision from formal authority, the law then in effect, contemporaneous information, feasible actions, constraints, incentives and observable consequences. Label later information as hindsight. Lawful explanations, misconduct hypotheses and data/identity errors must be allowed to compete; do not select a theory merely because a powerful actor benefited.

Translate suspected mechanisms into records that could distinguish them: original estimates, amendments, competitive bids, evaluation criteria, ownership on the operative date, declarations, payment/receipt references, acceptance, audit responses, recovery and legal dispositions. Do not turn those mechanisms into instructions for evading procurement, concealing payments or manipulating an investigation.

A missing hop remains missing. Same name, proximity, common ministry, repeated URL, shared address, meeting, donation or budget increase does not independently establish a transfer or coordinated wrongdoing. A financial write-off is not automatically a waiver, a sanction is not expenditure, a pledge is not a receipt, a penalty imposed is not cash recovered, and an attachment is not a final loss.
