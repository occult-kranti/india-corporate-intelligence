# Fifteen-year public-funding investigation panel

Review date: 8 October 2026, America/New_York. Scope: 8 October 2011–8 October 2026, with earlier events explicitly identified as background. Source retrieval timestamps remain in UTC.

The lead coordinates six parallel AI workstreams: defence procurement; police, border administration and public attack-attribution records; water and energy; welfare and PM CARES; other sector networks; and methods/data review. These are assigned analytical perspectives, not independent human testimony. The current local retrievers remain evidence-discovery tools; neither failed adapter is promoted or retrained during this investigation.

## Round one: evidence and decision reconstruction

Each specialist inventories previous evidence before acquiring current originals. The goal is a new documentary connection, an independently testable inconsistency, or a correction to an earlier claim—not a quota of accusations. Fiscal allocations, sanctions, awards, invoices, payments, alleged receipts, recoveries, attachments and ownership are different relations. The source-family and geographic scope accompany the record. A current report describing a 2016 decision is not proof that the report's information was available in 2016.

For selected decisions, the panel reconstructs formal authority, institutional incentives, constraints, available information, lawful and adverse possibilities, their distinct observable implications, and the later record. Qualitative game-theoretic reasoning supplies competing explanations; it does not supply private motives, numerical guilt scores or retrospective forecasts. An option that could have been available is not an allegation that it was chosen.

## Round two: challenge and integration

Each research file is assigned to another specialist for a challenge of its strongest adverse assertion, monetary figure, legal status, source independence, identity join and counterevidence. The lead reviews proposed cross-sector links separately. Rejected joins remain in the public research record. Budget increases, beneficiaries and failures of prevention do not establish that an attack was staged. Attack attribution, financing and procurement oversight require their own evidence chains.

## Coverage and deliverables

The research uses the existing atlas sectors, including finance, transport, health, education, NGOs, media, justice, natural resources and international finance. Fresh casework and inherited records have distinct review dates. An institution-level backlog shows what was examined, partly examined, queued or inaccessible; the 15-year window is not a claim that every year, force, state, tender or company has been investigated.

Deliverables are source-captured research packs, a machine-checked evidence graph, rejected-join and decision-analysis registers, reproducible read-only analysis tools, multiple acquisition and validation roadmaps, and a map-led website desk. Publication remains within the user's existing authorization. Source integrity, graph closure, mobile/keyboard flows and public deployment identity must be checked. Existing unrelated legacy acceptance failures remain disclosed rather than relabelled as passes.
