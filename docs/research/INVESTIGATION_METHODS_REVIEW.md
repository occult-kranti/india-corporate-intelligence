# Investigation methods review

Reviewed 14 October 2026. This is a methods review and an implementable research design, not a finding that any named administration or organization committed a hidden offence.

## What this review changes

The useful frontier is disciplined evidence handling, entity resolution and error detection. A forceful prompt, a plausible strategic motive, many mutually agreeing agents, or a large visual network cannot replace those operations. The proposed workflow makes a strong allegation easier to support if records warrant it, and easier to withdraw if they do not.

The literature is deliberately mixed: scholarly books and a peer-reviewed psychology review supply conceptual foundations; professional manuals supply practice; current preprints supply emerging evaluation ideas; primary provider documentation supplies data semantics. None of these categories is interchangeable. No comparative experiment establishes this assembled workflow as “state of the art.” Three 2026 preprints were actually opened, but their reported rankings were not reproduced or imported as local performance claims.

There are fourteen source entries and nine methods in `research/investigation-program/methods.json`. The sources include access depth, limitations and evidentiary family. `full-text` describes the acquired content, not a claim that every page of a book was read; selected sections are stated below. The Cambridge book entry is deliberately limited to its publisher summary.

## A research design that can change its mind

The unit of investigation is an exact proposition: actor, action, object, period, jurisdiction and mechanism. “Connected to” and “money involved” are too broad. For every proposition, keep a source-linked observation separate from interpretation. A claim that a refund occurred is different from a claim about why it occurred, and both differ from a claim about who benefited.

For a proposed mechanism, write rival predictions before broad acquisition. The alternatives should include ordinary institutional operation, administrative failure or measurement error when plausible, as well as a different misconduct mechanism. This is not a requirement to manufacture an equally strong rival to a directly established fact. It is a way to prevent a single narrative from determining which records count. A decisive contradiction should revise the hypothesis; it should not automatically become another sign of concealment.

A strategy worksheet can help identify records. Consider roles, legal authority, incentives, possible high-level choices and information constraints; then ask which observation would differ under compliant administration and the hypothesized abuse. Unknown payoffs stay unknown. Without a specified game, calibrated payoffs and defensible information assumptions, a toy incentive expression is not an estimated equilibrium. Prediction after reading the outcome must be labeled retrospective. The value of the exercise is a discriminating acquisition job, not a declaration of guilt.

Legal rules require their own dated source and jurisdiction. An audit objection, allegation, agency charge, lower-court finding, appeal and final disposition are different states. The relevant entity and count must travel with each status. A general rule name is insufficient to pronounce a violation.

## Separate identity, relationship and mechanism

A graph must not collapse three inferential steps:

1. **Identity:** do two records refer to the same legal entity or person?
2. **Relationship:** what precise dated role or transaction does a source assert?
3. **Mechanism:** does the supported relationship actually discriminate between explanations of the outcome?

Candidate matching may use normalized spelling or transliteration; adjudication must retain original values and conflicts. Same-name or shared-office matches remain candidates. Validate complete clusters, because pairwise decisions can generate inconsistent transitive merges. Version merges and splits. A current canonical ID is not a permanent historical truth.

Every graph edge needs a typed predicate, source locator, asserted date and relevant validity interval. A director is not automatically a beneficial owner. A shareholding path is not a payment trail. An intermediary address does not establish common control. An appointment in 2023 cannot, without earlier evidence, establish authority over a 2020 award. Unknown days remain unknown rather than becoming synthetic January dates.

For money, distinguish authorization, allocation, contract award, amendment, invoice, payment, refund, delivery and certified utilization. Keep currency, scale, period and accounting basis. Reconcile one scope first. Adding the award and its payments counts the same commitment twice; adding an annual component to a cumulative figure does likewise. A missing transaction bridge remains an explicit question even if other links are documented.

## Comparison populations and causal limits

A systemic claim requires a defined eligible population rather than a gallery of striking winners. For donation/procurement questions, a useful starting unit is bidder × tender × period, with eligibility, unsuccessful bids, exposure dates and award outcomes. Record the parts of the population absent from the public sources. A denominator of “companies we found in news stories” is not a denominator of all eligible companies.

The entity network describes actors and asserted relations. A causal diagram describes assumptions about how variables influence one another. Keep them separate. Firm size or sector can affect both political participation and procurement opportunity. Public visibility, auditing and investigation can affect whether cases enter a dataset. Restricting analysis to visible cases can therefore change the association. Controls should follow the proposed causal structure; adjusting for every available variable can create bias.

Before estimating an effect, specify the contrast, timing, outcome measurement, comparability and overlap assumptions. Where those conditions are not defensible, a transparent descriptive association plus a concrete acquisition plan is more informative than an unjustified causal coefficient. A complete cohort is necessary for some questions, but it does not by itself remove confounding or prove intent.

## Evidence retention and source dependence

Preserve what was actually acquired: requested and redirected URLs, retrieval time, access outcome, MIME type, byte hash and version where lawful. Keep OCR, translation and extracted tables as derivatives with their input hash and tool settings. Inspect original tables when their layout or footnotes affect interpretation. Hash equality shows byte identity; it cannot establish factual accuracy or completeness.

Separate source authentication, item integrity and content verification. Record the exact passage, row and version supporting each consequential statement and what that passage cannot establish. A news report repeating a filing, an aggregator containing the report and another agent quoting the aggregator share an origin. Count independent evidentiary families rather than domains, citations or agents. Official records may be authoritative for a decision while still being incomplete or disputed about underlying events.

Search access is a measured property. Record full text, visual PDF inspection, search excerpt, index or failed access. A failed link is not an unavailable fact across every archive. Public-data acquisition does not authorize authentication bypass, private-data harvesting or sending messages. Prepare requests where useful; transmit them only when authorized.

## Agent architecture and evaluation

Use a coordinator to define bounded questions and track the evidence ledger; give acquisition workers non-overlapping record sets or rival hypotheses. A verifier needs a fresh context with raw records and neutral questions, not the draft verdict. Compare its reconstruction with the draft only after it responds. Two models using one erroneous filing remain one evidentiary origin; context separation reduces a form of anchoring but does not establish statistical independence.

Assess research process separately from polished output. A useful forward-test packet has a known wrong identifier, a superseded filing, a unit mismatch, duplicated financial stages or a citation that fails to support its claim. Keep the key outside the worker prompt. Score false merges, missed contradictions, unsupported accusations, citation fidelity and appropriate abstention. A small local exercise is a smoke test, not a validated accuracy estimate. Recent evaluator research motivates these challenges; it does not justify trusting another LLM as the final arbiter.

Conspiracy-cognition research is a check on the investigator's pattern seeking, certainty and selective attention. It is not a diagnostic tool for the user, a target, or a population. Real coordination can be documented; a pejorative label cannot rebut records. Equally, the possibility of real conspiracies cannot make every coincidence probative. Apply the same evidentiary rules when the political identities are swapped. Dentith’s philosophical analysis adds a separate constraint: a reason to postpone a weakly supported theory is not necessarily evidence that it is false. The workflow therefore keeps “not yet supported,” “contradicted by records” and “not investigated” distinct. It also resists the reverse mistake of treating the possibility of suppressed evidence as affirmative support.

## Concrete application in this repository

The existing Game Theory Lab already collects questions, predictions, alternatives, falsifiers and requested records. Keep it as a hypothesis worksheet and attach: observation IDs, scope/cohort, rival predictions, original source locators, evidentiary families and review history. Retain versioned drafts. The research-program methods library can display which procedures are established practice, emerging adaptations or experimental local designs, including their limits.

The PM CARES acquisition completed in this session is a useful bounded example. The FY2024–25 cash rows reconcile. The public one-page statement refers to notes, including Note 11 for implementing-agency refunds, but those notes were not supplied by the inspected official link set. The current agency, project, origin period and reason for the refund therefore remain unresolved. A prior year's identifiable ventilator refund is not an identity key for the current aggregate. See `docs/research/PMCARES_NOTES_ACQUISITION.md` for exact URLs, hashes, searches and the stop boundary. No diversion finding follows from that gap.

Three high-value next jobs remain: acquire Note 11 or a project-level refund register with agency and origin period; assemble a complete bidder/tender comparison population before testing donation-linked outcomes; and update significant legal claims at case × party × count level using current dispositive orders. Each job should specify its exact custodian, period, fields and measurable success criterion. These are limited acquisition jobs, not assertions that missing evidence exists or is publicly obtainable.

## Sources actually inspected

The following list is the bibliography and read-depth register. The exact versioned URLs used by the site are in `methods.json`. Access date for all entries is 2026-10-14. Sources in the same family are not counted as independent confirmation of the same fact. Family boundaries are claim-specific: the Hanson quality-control material in Story-Based Inquiry chapter 8 and the GIJN guide share a methodological lineage, so citing both is not independent validation of that editorial procedure.

1. **[Story-Based Inquiry: A Manual for Investigative Journalists, second edition](https://www.storybasedinquiry.com/manual2)** — UNESCO / Mark Lee Hunter and contributors; manual; `full-text`. Provisional hypotheses, event chronology and source-linked editorial checking. Actual 188-page PDF obtained from author-linked public download. Read selected chapter 2, 3 and 8 passages, not the whole book. PDF frontmatter says 2026; author landing page says 2025. Family: `unesco-storybasedinquiry`.

2. **[Berkeley Protocol on Digital Open Source Investigations](https://humanrights.berkeley.edu/wp-content/uploads/archive/2024/02/Berkeley-Protocol.pdf)** — OHCHR / UC Berkeley Human Rights Center; manual; `full-text`. Preservation and verification of source, digital item and content; independent corroboration. Read selected preservation, verification, analysis and bias paragraphs, especially 161–200. Human-rights protocol; its procedural safeguards do not establish any allegation here. Family: `ohchr-berkeley`.

3. **[Causal Inference: What If — January 2025 revision](https://miguelhernan.org/s/hernanrobins_WhatIf_2jan25.pdf)** — Miguel A. Hernán / James M. Robins, author-hosted book; scholarly-book; `full-text`. Distinguishing confounding from selection bias; conditions for identifying causal effects. Read selected chapters 7 and 8 in the full author PDF. No causal effect estimated for this project. Book examples are mainly epidemiological. Family: `hernan-robins`.

4. **[A Guide to Fact-Checking Investigative Stories](https://gijn.org/resource/guide-to-fact-checking-investigative-stories/)** — GIJN / Nils Hanson; professional-guide; `full-text`. Line-by-line checking, contrary evidence and attention to the narrative frame. Professional practice guidance, not a controlled validation study. Published responses can be checked; contacting subjects requires authorization. Family: `gijn-hanson`.

5. **[OpenSanctions Pairs: Large-Scale Entity Matching with LLMs](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.11051v1)** — Smith, Sesodia, Lindenberg and Schroeder de Witt / arXiv; preprint; `full-text`. Pairwise entity matching evaluation and errors involving identifiers, scripts and incomplete records. Read dataset, matching, evaluation and limitation sections. Candidate pairs are selected by blocking; labels reflect analyst decisions. Pairwise results do not validate entire graphs or Indian registries. Family: `opensanctions-pairs-research`.

6. **[Time to Reflect: Can We Trust LLM Judges for Evidence-based Research Agents?](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.19196v1)** — Wang and coauthors / arXiv; preprint; `full-text`. Testing evaluators with controlled errors in research trajectories and evidence use. Read task construction, judge setup and limitations. Synthetic perturbations do not cover all naturally occurring failures; no local benchmark reproduced. Family: `reflect-research`.

7. **[Towards a Science of AI Agent Reliability, version 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.16666v3)** — Rabanser and coauthors / arXiv; preprint; `full-text`. Reliability dimensions beyond average task success: consistency, robustness, predictability and failure severity. Read abstract, framework and evaluation overview, not every appendix. Results concern the studied tasks and models; they do not establish reliability for this investigation. Family: `agent-reliability-research`.

8. **[The Psychology of Conspiracy Theories](https://kar.kent.ac.uk/61995/1/Douglas%20Sutton%20Cichocka%202017.pdf)** — Douglas, Sutton and Cichocka / Current Directions in Psychological Science; peer-reviewed-review; `full-text`. Review of epistemic, existential and social motives relevant to investigator self-scrutiny. Read the article text in the author repository PDF. Population-level research cannot diagnose individuals or decide whether a particular allegation is true. Family: `douglas-sutton-cichocka`.

9. **[Offshore Leaks Database: Frequently Asked Questions](https://offshoreleaks.icij.org/pages/faq)** — International Consortium of Investigative Journalists; dataset-documentation; `full-text`. Dataset-specific coverage, transformations, duplicate identities and derived similar-name/address links. Read FAQ sections on coverage, transformations and interpretation. Database presence is not evidence of an offence; records are historical and incomplete. Family: `icij-offshoreleaks`.

10. **[Beneficial Ownership Data Standard 0.4: Key Concepts](https://standard.openownership.org/en/latest/primer/concepts.html)** — Open Ownership / Open Data Services; standard; `full-text`. Separating source assertions from real-world facts and keeping dated, append-only ownership statements. Read concepts page, not complete schema or jurisdictional ownership law. A conforming record can still contain an inaccurate declaration. Family: `openownership-bods`.

11. **[OpenSanctions: Statement Data Model](https://www.opensanctions.org/docs/statements/)** — OpenSanctions; technical-documentation; `full-text`. Field-level source lineage and separate source and canonical identifiers. Read full page. First/last observed dates are pipeline observations; they need not be the dates a relationship legally began or ended. Licensing must be checked before reuse. Family: `opensanctions-documentation`.

12. **[OpenSanctions: Identifiers and Deduplication](https://www.opensanctions.org/docs/identifiers/)** — OpenSanctions; technical-documentation; `full-text`. Canonical ID changes, merge/split history and limits of consolidated profiles. Read full page. Vendor documentation describes its system, not validated accuracy for a local importer; software openness and dataset permissions differ. Family: `opensanctions-documentation`.

13. **[Process Tracing: From Metaphor to Analytic Tool, chapter 1](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/process-tracing/process-tracing/A2843D39550F9A39377768A88D266A82)** — Andrew Bennett / Jeffrey T. Checkel, Cambridge University Press; scholarly-book-chapter; `index-only`. Publisher summary identifies competing causal mechanisms as the book chapter’s concern. Publisher summary and bibliographic record read only. SFU working-paper link denied access and another author chapter timed out; no claim to have read those chapters. Family: `bennett-checkel`.

14. **[Suspicious conspiracy theories](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11229-022-03602-4)** — M R. X. Dentith, Synthese (2022); peer-reviewed philosophy; `full-text`. Publisher article opened and introduction plus sections 6–8 inspected. Distinguishes preliminary grounds for suspicion and research prioritization from evidential assessment of an individual theory. This is one position in an active philosophical debate, not an empirical accuracy result. Family: `dentith-epistemology`.
