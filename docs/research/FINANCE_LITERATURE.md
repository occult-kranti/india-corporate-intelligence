# External finance to India — the literature, the records and the narratives

*Annotated bibliography, verified 2026-09-26. Machine-readable twin: `research/raw/finance/literature.json`.
Every entry was checked against an opened publisher, dataset, court, lender or encyclopaedia page; where that
failed the entry says so. Web search was capped for this session, so the sweep worked from known URLs;
imf.org, dea.gov.in, ieg.worldbankgroup.org and cag.gov.in's search were unreachable from this environment.*

**How to read the reliability notes.** Three genres answer three questions. *Lender records* (OED/IEG
evaluations, program documents, internal audits) establish what the lender itself found — they are candid
about failure and silent about motive. *Memoirs of office-holders* (Ahluwalia, Rangarajan, Reddy) are
primary on sequence and atmosphere and self-interested on credit. *Peer-reviewed work* (Kentikelenis &
Stubbs, Dreher et al., Fazekas & Kocsis) supplies base rates and method and almost never names an Indian
beneficiary. Money is stated in ₹ crore with the conversion given; every conversion here is approximate
and flagged as such because none of the sources opened gave a rupee figure.

## 5. Symmetry check

The brief's controls, run through the same lens:

- **UPA vs NDA.** The strongest documented case of external conditions shaping Indian law is 1991, under Congress — and the Bank's evaluator says even then conditionality was "used sparingly". NDA-era DPOs carry prior actions drawn from the government's own programmes. The "strings" lens detects crisis-year borrowing, not party.
- **BJP-run vs opposition-run states.** The one litigated Union restriction (Kerala 2023–24) sits beside a 2019 IDA-blend DPO to the same opposition-run state; the Bank's 1990s focus states were governed by six different parties. No partisan pattern is documented; the analytic gap stays open until the state-wise EAP table is built from `worldbank-projects.json`.
- **Chinese vs Japanese money.** The "closed club" and "capture" lenses run on the Japanese control produce a literal contractual closed club (tied MAHSR procurement) and a 50-year creditor relationship with a Union PSU — more concentrated than anything documented for Chinese credit to Indian private firms. The alarm the lens raises for China is a property of the lens unless control rights are shown.
- **Doing Business.** The countries named are China, Azerbaijan, Saudi Arabia and the UAE. India is reviewed and cleared.

## 6. Voids (documented absences)

1. No IEG country-programme evaluation of the Bank in India after the 2001 CAE was returned by the Documents API — the lender's country-level verdict is 25 years old.
2. The GIA verification report lists no India irregularity; India's two items are recorded as reviewed and cleared.
3. The OED attributes Indian procurement failure to weak bid documents and award delays — no finding of collusion or a closed pool.
4. Article 293 had "not been so far the subject of an authoritative pronouncement" of the Supreme Court as of 2024-04-01.
5. No CAG audit of externally aided projects could be located (cag.gov.in search 404).
