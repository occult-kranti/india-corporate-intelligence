# Olympus research update: satellites, university funding and Bitcoin

17 October 2026. Selected, source-reviewed findings. This is an initial set of documentary paths, not a complete global network or proof of a conspiracy. The new findings have not been added to the published graph.

## Satellite technology and procurement

- **Starshield:** SpaceX's June 2026 prospectus describes government Earth observation, secure communications and hosted payloads drawing on Starlink infrastructure. This is an issuer description, not a verified inventory of every satellite's equipment. [Prospectus, printed p103](https://content.spacex.com/cms-assets/FINAL_Documents%20and%20Updates/SpaceX%20-%20EU%20Prospectus%20(Approved%20by%20Bafin)%20-%20June%205,%202026.pdf)
- **A concrete procurement trail:** SDA announced contract HQ0850-21-C-0001 to SpaceX for $149,175,246 on October 5, 2020. GAO later reported SpaceX–York optical-link demonstrations using Tesat terminals, while wider integration remained incomplete. Award value, supplier relationship and demonstrated capability are separate facts; a complete payment trail is still missing. [SDA award](https://www.sda.mil/sda-awards-contracts-for-the-first-generation-of-the-tracking-layer/), [GAO report](https://files.gao.gov/reports/GAO-26-107085/index.html)
- **Ukraine terminal transfers:** USAID OIG's itemized account describes 5,000 terminals transferred through DAI to SSSCIP, plus 175 sent separately. It repeatedly records 3,667 donated terminals, but the introduction says 3,677. The discrepancy is in the original PDF. Monitoring gaps and agency disagreement are documented; intentional diversion is not established. Recommendation status at report issuance is not current status. [OIG report, pp2–3 and5–10](https://oig.usaid.gov/sites/default/files/2025-09/%5BREDACTED%5D%20Final%20Report%20-%20Inspection%20of%20USAID%27s%20Oversight%20of%20Starlink%20Terminals%20Delivered%20to%20Ukraine.pdf)

The proposed FCC Gen2 authorization claim stays withheld because the original order was not inspected. Missing payload details do not establish exotic or clandestine technology.

## University funding and the DCI lead

MIT's investigation reports $850,000 in Epstein gifts during 2002–2017. Its $525,000 Media Lab subtotal covers distinct discretionary, Bach and Oxman support; it is **not itself a Digital Currency Initiative allocation**. A reported 2015 DCI-related meeting is a separate relationship. [MIT investigation, pp5,10 and49](https://web.mit.edu/fact2020/files/MIT-report.pdf)

A two-page DOJ-hosted email reproduction, visually inspected after the authorized age check, identifies an April 25, 2015 Ito-to-Epstein message saying gift funds underwrote DCI. It supplies no amount, legal donor vehicle, transfer record or grant restrictions. This is a qualified funding lead requiring reconciliation with university accounts, not authenticated original correspondence or evidence of Bitcoin control. [EFTA00680068](https://www.justice.gov/epstein/files/DataSet%209/EFTA00680068.pdf)

DCI separately reports $4 million in charitable contributions for a **2020–2024** security program. Contributor names and adjacent employer affiliations must not be converted into corporate donor identities without gift records. No per-contributor allocation is supplied. [DCI program](https://www.dci.mit.edu/bitcoin-security-initiative)

Harvard's review reports $9.179 million in Epstein gifts during 1998–2007. It documents later introductions and facilitation, but says the review did not establish that introduced donors' money came from Epstein. Donor denials and corroboration for one Black gift must remain visible. Gifts, pledges and legal donor vehicles cannot be merged. [Harvard review, pp3 and13–15](https://ogc.harvard.edu/files/ogc/files/report_concerning_jeffrey_e._epsteins_connections_to_harvard_university.pdf)

## Separate Bitcoin growth map: reviewed research base

The first dataset contains **32 selected timeline events, 22 entities/concepts and 16 typed relationships**, with qualified claims kept explicit. It covers selected precursors, Bitcoin launches and protocol milestones, institutional custody, corporate financing and regulation. It is not complete cryptocurrency history.

The retained [Coin Metrics Community Data](https://gitbook-docs.coinmetrics.io/packages/coin-metrics-community-data) provides **6,496 consecutive daily observations, January 3, 2009–October 16, 2026**, and 214 calculated monthly summaries. Independent recalculation reproduced the monthly aggregates. Six initial hash-rate observations are null, and the first and latest months are partial. These are provider measurements, not an independently operated full-node census.

The board will keep these layers distinct:
- Global activity, estimated hash rate and ledger supply. Addresses are not people; transactions are not necessarily payments.
- Dated legal jurisdictions, institutional roles and public disclosures.
- Explicit missing geography: no current mining-country or reachable-node dataset was acquired.

Three deeper trails are staged: common institutional custodians; Strategy financing versus disclosed Bitcoin purchases; and government seizure, forfeiture and reserve directions. Missing transaction or custody bridges remain unlinked. The reserve order does not supply a verified current balance or establish that a particular seized balance entered the reserve. [IBIT annual filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1980994/000143774926006058/bit20251231_10k.htm), [Strategy annual filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1050446/000105044626000020/mstr-20251231.htm), [reserve order](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/03/establishment-of-the-strategic-bitcoin-reserve-and-united-states-digital-asset-stockpile/)

Important review corrections retained in the map's separate input projection:
- China notice document date is not a verified signing date.
- El Salvador is the IMF program/borrower country, not an asserted physical approval location.
- A verified block header at Taproot's minimum activation height does not independently verify earlier signaling lock-in.
- MiCA exceptions and transition limits remain attached to application dates.
- A purchase disclosure date is not independently verified settlement timing.
- Trusts, sponsors, parents and custodians are different legal roles; availability of a custodian is not an observed transfer.
