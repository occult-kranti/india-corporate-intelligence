# Energy & resources — the literature, the documents and the narratives

*Annotated bibliography, verified 2026-09-25. Machine-readable twin: `research/raw/energy/literature.json`.
Every entry below was checked against an opened publisher, court, regulator or encyclopaedia page; where that
failed the entry says so. Nothing here is cited from memory without a flag.*

**How to read the reliability notes.** Three genres answer three different questions. *Official records*
(CAG, Supreme Court, SEC/DOJ, parliamentary committees) establish facts but not motive and, in the case of
indictments and short-seller reports, are allegations until adjudicated. *Peer-reviewed work* explains
structure and almost never names a beneficiary. *Investigative books and reports* name beneficiaries and
carry the litigation risk. Silence in one genre says nothing about the others.

## 5. Symmetry check

Run the same lens (audit → book → legal reprisal → "favoured national champion") on Reliance under the UPA
(2004–14) instead of Adani under the NDA. It produces the same shape: CAG audit (KG-D6, 2011), investigative
book (*Gas Wars*, 2014), ₹100 crore defamation demand, an opposition narrative of "Ambani's government". Two
asymmetries are findings about institutions, not firms: under UPA the damaging primary records came from
domestic institutions (CAG 2011, CAG 2012, SC 2014); under NDA the damaging records on Adani are foreign
(Hindenburg, OCCRP/FT, SEC/DOJ) while domestic institutions (SEBI, SC, Lokpal) have so far produced records
cutting the other way. And the 2014 coal cancellation covered NDA-1 allocations too. Applied to a third
control — Tata, JSW, Vedanta, who also won ports, airports, coal and renewables under both governments — the
lens generates no comparable literature: the literature follows the two largest promoters, not the policy
instruments.

## 6. Voids (documented absences)

- No court, SEBI, CAG or ED record finds Adani to be a proxy or financing vehicle for the BJP.
- The March 2024 bond disclosures, as summarised, link no bond purchase to any coal block or energy contract.
- No CAG audit of PM CARES exists; donors undisclosed.
- No Standing Committee on Energy report (per PRS listing) on the 2022 coal-import mandate or the SECI
  manufacturing-linked tender.
- Adani's denials of the OCCRP (2023) and FT coal-grade (2024) stories, and SEBI's September 2025 orders,
  were not captured on our side.
