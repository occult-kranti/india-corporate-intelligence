# Welfare politics and cash transfers — the literature, the documents and the narratives

*Annotated bibliography, verified 2026-09-25. Machine-readable twin: `research/raw/welfare/literature.json`.
Every entry was checked against an opened publisher record (Crossref metadata for DOIs), an official page, a court
record or a locally held PDF retrieved by a sibling agent; where that failed the entry says so. The web-search budget
was exhausted before this sweep began, so verification ran on direct fetches only. Nothing below is cited from memory
without a flag.*

**How to read the reliability notes.** Four genres answer four different questions. *Survey political science*
(Lokniti-CSDS, Deshpande–Tillin–Kailash, Sircar) tells you who voted how and what they say they received; it cannot
by itself separate a scheme's effect from selection. *RCT economics* (Muralidharan, Niehaus, Sukhtankar; Banerjee et
al.) tells you whether a delivery reform cut leakage or exclusion; it has never, in India, measured votes. *Fiscal
monitors* (RBI, PRS, Economic Survey) tell you what the schemes cost and what they crowd out; they do not measure
welfare. *Courts* tell you what is lawful, not what is wise. The international literature is the only place the
cash-to-votes question has been tested causally, and even there the answer is contested (Imai et al. 2020).

## 8. The ten narratives on the six-step ladder

*established → well-supported → contested → speculative → unsupported → debunked.* Full strongest-case / strongest-counter / what-would-change text is in the JSON; this is the verdict line.

| # | Narrative | Verdict | One-line reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cash transfers buy elections | **contested** | Positive effects abroad (5/6) but Imai et al.'s null, scheme-specific signs in India (Ujjwala +, MGNREGA −), and zero Indian causal estimates. |
| 2 | Ladli Behna decided MP 2023 | **contested** | 80% coverage leaves no counterfactual; no beneficiary × vote table published; Congress matched the offer. |
| 3 | Freebies are bankrupting the states | **contested** | ₹1.68 lakh crore is 0.5% of GDP and 6% of revenue expenditure; committed expenditure is 53%; six of 12 UCT states in revenue deficit — a real pressure, not bankruptcy. |
| 4 | The revdi speech was hypocrisy | **contested** | BJP states launched four women's cash schemes after the speech; the speech itself exempts houses, gas, toilets; 12 states of all parties do the same. |
| 5 | DBT eliminated leakage | **contested** | AP smartcards RCT is strong; "eliminated" and the ₹5.14 lakh crore "estimated gains" are self-reported; Jharkhand shows exclusion costs. |
| 6 | Women's cash schemes are empowerment, not vote-buying | **contested** | False dichotomy; courts hold them lawful; no outcome evaluation exists; launch timing and post-poll cuts are documented. |
| 7 | Schemes are cut once the election is won | **contested** | Maharashtra fits (₹2,100 promised, ₹500 for overlap); Jharkhand, Assam, WB, MP do not. |
| 8 | Central schemes are branded to the PM to buy loyalty | **contested** | Attribution shift is documented (Deshpande et al.); motive is inference; states brand identically. |
| 9 | Opposition states are starved of central funds (MGNREGA in WB) | **speculative** | Not doubted, but **no primary record opened** in this sweep; PIB releases are silent on Bengal. |
| 10 | The freebies debate is a class attack on the poor | **contested** | Asymmetric labelling is observable; the fiscal argument stands on its own; the anchor texts (Ghosh) were not located. |

## 9. Voids and gaps

- No experimental or quasi-experimental estimate of any Indian cash scheme's effect on votes (0 of 8).
- The public Lokniti MP 2023 findings PDF has no gender × vote or beneficiary × vote table.
- No primary record on the West Bengal MGNREGA stoppage (MoRD s.27; Calcutta HC) was opened.
- No independent outcome evaluation of Ladli Behna or Ladki Bahin located.
- RBI's June 2022 *State Finances: A Risk Analysis* and the 2024-25 State Finances PDF not opened; RBI enters only via PRS.
- "Techno-patrimonialism" (Aiyar & Tillin, Seminar) not verified; Aiyar & Walton not verified; Ghatak & Muralidharan (IPF) not found on Crossref.
- Lokniti Karnataka 2023 and West Bengal 2021 post-polls not located; Jayati Ghosh's freebies texts not located; NITI Aayog unreachable.
