# Foreign capital in listed India — the literature, the documents and the narratives

*Annotated bibliography, verified 2026-09-26. Machine-readable twin: `research/raw/capital/narratives-literature.json`
(every work marked "Yes" below is a node or a source there). Every entry was checked against an opened publisher,
regulator, court or newsroom page through this session's proxy; where that failed the entry says so and nothing from
it is cited in the graph. Nothing here is cited from memory without a flag.*

**How to read the reliability notes.** Four genres answer four different questions. *Regulatory and judicial records*
(SEBI orders, Supreme Court judgments, the Lokpal) establish what was and was not found — under a specific regulation,
at a specific date — and never motive. *Short-seller and investigative reports* (Hindenburg, OCCRP) name beneficiaries,
are interested or adversarial by construction, and are allegations until adjudicated; their factual substrate (bank
records, filings) can be true even when the regulator finds no violation. *Party statements* (BJP, Congress) are
themselves the narratives under calibration. *Academic and official-economics work* (BIS, WID, treaty scholarship)
explains structure and almost never names an actor. A finding in one genre says nothing about the others.
