# Selected case workspace: review candidate

An explicit **Selected case files** action opens a separate case workspace inside the felt atlas. The initial cohort is **19 selected cases and 72 core claims**, with 91 case-local nodes, 73 authored relation roles and 13 reader-only facts. It is selected-case coverage, not a worldwide wealth census. Public-office profiles keep that role distinction.

## Reading and scene behavior

Opening the chooser loads only its small UI and index. Choosing a case lazily loads one reviewed data transport and the complete accessible reader. The user can then choose **Case scene** on a supported desktop viewport. This draws physical paper cards, pins and restrained threads on a local felt surface, using the atlas's existing canvas, WebGL context and Three renderer. It has an independent case camera and does not place those cards on world coordinates.

Case jurisdiction is a source-qualified research label. It does not establish a residence, headquarters, operational site, geocoded building or canonical global entity join. The global atlas's accepted factual graph and raw registry do not gain any of these claims or paths. Typed and qualified roles retain their exact source direction, capacities, historical dates and financial stages; reader-only facts never gain threads.

Select a pin or relation to focus its local neighborhood. Complete reading, sources, qualifiers, exclusions and remaining eligible-role counts remain accessible. Pan/zoom is explicit; the camera can pause or be interrupted. Close stops case travel and restores the captured geographic camera and enabled input handlers. It never resumes an interrupted geographic journey. Source-dialog Escape closes that dialog first; the next Escape closes the workspace. Close controls occupy a separate band above keyboard-scrollable evidence.

Reduced motion disables animated travel. Compact viewports, unavailable WebGL and failed scene imports retain the complete reader. The controller, selected evidence and source dialog live outside disposable graphics. A fatal shared-renderer error requires explicit full graphics recreation; no later Three pass may reuse the poisoned renderer. No automatic speech, source transmission or camera journey is added.

The existing optional local-voice narration and worldwide estimated-building overlays remain atlas features. The case workspace does not yet add narration of the new case files or its 18 dated wealth observations. A deferred, explicitly opened wealth view with source/date/non-cash-estimate labels remains follow-on work.
