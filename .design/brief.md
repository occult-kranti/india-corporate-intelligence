# ICIP geographic investigation workspace

User direction: geographic India map linked to a relationship graph; map layout
across all pages; a deeper platform for investigating public functions, funding,
people, legal proceedings, public reports, PM CARES, welfare and corporate debt.

Primary pattern: explore, investigate and compare. Replace the article-first
landing surfaces with a dense but readable work surface: geography, connected
entities and source evidence remain close together. Preserve full original
sector dossiers as a deliberate mode, deep links and saved work.

Visual direction: precise analytical workspace, restrained dark canvas, clear
state boundaries, consistent semantic colours/shapes, compact numerical labels
and conspicuous active scope. No promotional hero, decorative metrics, implied
corruption ranking or giant unbounded node cloud.

Complete the core journey: place→layer→entity/path→record/source→casebook→export.
Make national context, unknown location, legal status, amount stage and evidence
limitations visible where the user makes an interpretation. Mobile recomposes
into map/graph/evidence/casebook/dossier surfaces with persistent selection.

## Superseding visual direction — 7 October 2026

The authorized Public Record Atlas replacement is specified in `.design/atlas-brief.md` and token-bearing `DESIGN.md`.
